# NDIS registration cost in 2026-27: what a small provider really pays

**The short answer: the audit is the big cost, and the audit type decides it.** A verification audit for lower-risk supports is quoted in the low thousands or under. A certification audit, with a site visit, is quoted in the thousands. Documents and help are on top, and that is where prices range from tens of dollars to many thousands.

Every figure below says where it comes from and when it was read. Prices change; check the source before you budget.

Auditors quote per provider, so your own quotes will differ. Get at least two.

## What drives the number

- **The audit type.** Lower-risk groups only: verification. Any higher-risk group: certification, with a site visit. The questionnaire tells you which, with the Commission's list as the source.
- **How ready your documents are.** Auditors charge for time. Documents that already match the standards shorten the audit.
- **How many sites and workers you have.** A certification auditor samples workers and participants; more of each means more time.
- **The mid-term audit.** Certified providers pay for a second, smaller audit about 18 months in. Budget for it.

## Where small providers overspend

- Paying a consultant AUD 2,500 or more for documents when the groups only need verification.
- Buying a template pack and then paying someone to tailor it anyway.
- Paying a year of software for something needed once every three years.
- Instalment plans and buy-now-pay-later on compliance products. We do not offer them; a fixed price, paid once, is cheaper and simpler.

## FAQ

### Is there a fee to apply to the Commission?

The Commission does not charge an application fee. You pay the approved quality auditor you choose; the Commission does not set audit prices.

(Source: the Australian Government's ABLIS entry for NDIS provider registration, https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/ag/ndis-provider-registration/40940, read 13 October 2026. That is a government page rather than the Commission's own, which does not mention a fee either way.)

### Why do audit quotes vary so much?

The Commission does not set audit prices. Each approved auditor quotes on your scope, sites, workers and readiness, so two quotes for the same provider can differ by thousands.

### Can I avoid the audit?

Not if you register. Registration needs an audit by an approved quality auditor. Unregistered providers can work only with self-managed and plan-managed participants, and that is shrinking as registration becomes compulsory in waves.
