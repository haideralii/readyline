# The registers an auditor asks for, and what each one proves

**The short answer: policies are one third of an audit; records are the rest.** The Commission says the auditor wants real examples, not just the policy: incident reports, complaints records, training logs, supervision notes. A perfect policy with an empty register is a finding. A good practice with nothing written down cannot be shown.

This guide lists the records auditors ask for, what each one proves, and where a one-to-ten-worker provider keeps it. The list of twelve comes from auditors' and consultants' own accounts (sector webinars, 2026), so it is practice, not a rule. What the Rules and the Practice Standards require is marked.

## Two records the Rules require you to keep

| Record              | What the rule says                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Where to keep it                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Incident register   | The Incident Management and Reportable Incidents Rules 2018 require an incident management system with records of incidents and of what you did. Reportable incidents must be notified to the Commission within the Rules' time frames. | Readyline's incident register: category, how serious, reportable or not, the date to notify the Commission by, and a trail of what happened since. |
| Complaints register | The Complaints Management and Resolution Rules 2018 require a complaints management and resolution system with records of complaints and how they were dealt with.                                                                      | Readyline's complaints register: how it arrived, who raised it by code, acknowledged, resolved, closed.                                            |

## The records auditors ask for in practice

These are not named in the Rules, but auditors ask for them because they show the system is alive. Keep them simple; a page each is enough for a small provider.

| Record                        | What it proves                                                                                             | Where to keep it                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Document control register     | Which version of each policy is current, when it was adopted and when it is next reviewed.                 | Readyline's policy review register covers adoption and review dates; a document control register built from your pack is being added. |
| Record of decisions           | That the owner or the key personnel decided things on purpose and wrote them down.                         | A page in your folder today; being added to Readyline's registers.                                                                    |
| Participant risk register     | Risk management per participant: how much each person relies on you and what happens if you cannot attend. | A line per participant by reference code, in your own file; being added to Readyline's registers.                                     |
| Conflict of interest register | That interests were declared and managed, with review dates.                                               | A page in your folder today; being added.                                                                                             |
| Delegations register          | Who acts when the owner or key person is away.                                                             | A page in your folder today, or the structure and delegations table in your governance policy; being added.                           |
| Improvement register          | That incidents, complaints and feedback led to a change, and the change was checked.                       | A page in your folder today; being added.                                                                                             |
| Onboarding checklists         | That each worker and each participant was taken through what they needed, signed and dated.                | Your pack's induction procedure and service agreement template; being added.                                                          |

## How the auditor reads a register

The Commission's description of an audit day says the auditor traces one thing end to end: an incident from the first report to the review to the notification. So the auditor does not count rows. They pick one and follow it.

That is why a register row needs a date, a code for the person, what happened, what you did, and what changed. Readyline's registers ask for those and nothing that would identify a participant: a code, never a name, a date of birth or a health detail.

## Keep them as things happen

Every register above is easier to keep on the day than to rebuild before the audit. Add the row when the thing happens, with the date it happened and the date you recorded it. An auditor can tell a register written in one afternoon.

## FAQ

### Do I need all twelve as a sole trader?

The Rules require an incident register and a complaints register. The Practice Standards that apply to you decide the rest. A sole trader's delegations register can be one line: who you have asked to contact participants if you are unwell.

### Can I keep registers on paper?

Yes. The Commission asks for records, not software. Paper or a spreadsheet is fine if it is dated, complete and kept safe. Readyline's registers add the reminders and the audit trail.

### Do the registers hold participant names?

They should not need to. Use a code for each person and keep the list of codes in your own records. The Commission's position on AI and digital tools says participant information should not go into tools without safeguards; codes keep it out.

### What does "reportable" mean in the incident register?

An incident that the Incident Management and Reportable Incidents Rules 2018 say must be notified to the Commission, within the time frames the Rules set. Your incident policy carries the categories and the time frames; the register flags the row.

### How far back does an auditor look?

The whole registration period for a renewal, and since the last audit for a mid-term audit. For a first registration, the auditor looks at what you have set up and any records since you started.

### What if a register is empty?

Say so, with the date you started it. An empty complaints register for a new provider is normal. An empty incident register for a provider with ten workers and fifty participants is a question.
