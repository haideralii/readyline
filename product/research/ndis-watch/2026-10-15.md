## What the instruments say

### Incident Rules

s 12(2): "The incident management system of a registered NDIS provider must provide for the following details, as a minimum, to be recorded in relation to each incident that occurs: (a) a description of the incident, including the impact on, or harm caused to, any person with disability affected by the incident; (b) whether the incident is a reportable incident; (c) if known—the time, date and place at which the incident occurred; (d) …the time and date the incident was first identified; (e) the names and contact details of the persons involved in the incident; (f) the names and contact details of any witnesses to the incident; …". Section 12 sets what is recorded, not how long it is kept.

s 20(1): applies where the provider "becomes aware that a reportable incident has occurred" and it is "(i) the death of a person with disability; or (ii) the serious injury of a person with disability; or (iii) the abuse or neglect of a person with disability; or (iv) the unlawful sexual or physical contact with, or assault of, a person with disability; or (v) sexual misconduct committed against, or in the presence of, a person with disability, including grooming of the person for sexual activity." s 20(2): "the registered NDIS provider must notify the Commissioner of the following information within 24 hours".

s 21(1): applies where the reportable incident "is not of a kind covered by paragraph 20(1)(b)". s 21(2): "The notification must: (a) be given in writing; and (b) be given within 5 business days after the provider became aware that the reportable incident occurred".

Reading for item 19: the use of a restrictive practice that is not authorised or not in a behaviour support plan is the s 21 kind (5 business days) unless the same event is also a s 20 kind, that is, it caused serious injury or is itself abuse or neglect, in which case s 20 (24 hours) applies to it. The app's rule (D-902) keeps "medium or high severity" as the harm proxy for the 24-hour window; the words now say what the Rules say.

s 25: "(1) If a registered NDIS provider becomes aware that a reportable incident has occurred …, the provider must keep a record of the incident. (2) The record must be kept for 7 years from the day that notification of the reportable incident is given under subsection 21(2)(b) or subsection 23(2) (as the case requires). … (3) It is sufficient compliance with this section if the provider keeps the record of the reportable incident required to be made under section 12."

### Complaints Rules

s 10(2): "The system must provide that appropriate records of complaints received by the registered NDIS provider are kept by the provider, including the following, where appropriate: (a) information about complaints; (b) any action taken to resolve complaints; (c) the outcome of any action taken." s 10(3): "A record made for the purposes of subsection (2) must be kept for 7 years from the day the record is made."

### Worker Screening Rules

s 13: a provider "must only allow a worker to engage in a risk assessed role, if the worker has a clearance" (subject to the exceptions in the Division). s 14(1)(a): a person may engage in a risk assessed role without a clearance only if "(i) the person is in the process of obtaining a clearance; (ii) the person is appropriately supervised by a person with a clearance; (iii) the registered NDIS provider is implementing a risk management plan …; (iv) the law of the participating jurisdiction … allows" it. s 15(2): a person is not in the process of obtaining a clearance "(a) if … on the most recent prior occasion the person was subject to an exclusion; … (e) on any day when an interim bar is in force in relation to the person; (f) at any time the person is not being supervised in accordance with the requirements of section 14".

s 18(3): the provider's written list of workers in risk assessed roles must include "(d) if a registered NDIS provider may only allow the worker to engage in a risk assessed role with a clearance: (i) the worker's NDIS worker screening check application number; and (ii) the worker's NDIS worker screening check number; and (iii) the worker's NDIS worker screening check outcome and any expiry date for that outcome; and (iv) whether the worker's clearance is subject to a decision to suspend or revoke a clearance …". So the record must hold the check number: item 22's optional field is added.

s 20: the list "in a form which would allow an auditor to determine which workers were engaged in risk assessed roles with the provider on any given day in the seven years prior to the day the auditor examines the list." s 21: "A record subject to this Part must be kept for 7 years from the date the record is made."

### Provider Registration and Practice Standards Rules

s 13(1): the provider "must give the Commissioner notice of any of the following changes: (a) a change to any of the provider's contact details …; (b) a change in the scale of the provider …, including … (i) a change to the geographical area or areas …; (ii) a change to the locations …; (iii) a significant increase or decrease in the number of participants …; (iv) a significant increase or decrease in the number of workers". s 13(2): "in the form approved by the Commissioner; and (b) as soon as practicable after the earlier of … when the provider becomes aware that the change will occur; … when the change occurs."

s 13A(1): notice of "(a) an event that significantly affects the provider's ability to comply with any of the provider's conditions of registration; (b) a change that adversely affects access by a person with disability to the supports or services …; (c) an adverse change in the provider's financial capacity …; (d) a significant change in the organisation or governance arrangements of the provider; (e) an event, relating to the suitability of the provider …; (f) an event, relating to the suitability of the provider's key personnel …". s 13A(2): "in the form approved by the Commissioner." s 13A(3): a change of ownership is notified "by the earlier of … when the provider becomes aware that the change in ownership will occur; (b) when a step or procedure occurs that is a necessary precondition to effecting the change of ownership." s 13A(4): any other event "by the earlier of … when the provider becomes aware that the event or change will occur; (b) when the event or change occurs."

Not found in ss 13 to 13A: a duty worded as "ceasing supports"; the nearest is s 13A(1)(b), a change that adversely affects access to the supports. The fixed text says that, not "ceasing". The approved forms are named only as "the Commission's approved form" because the Commission's site could not be read.

s 13B(4): the mid-term audit for certification providers "must commence no later than (a) 18 months after the beginning of the period for which the provider's registration is in force" (confirms REQ-009's 18-month constant used by batch 45 item 8).
