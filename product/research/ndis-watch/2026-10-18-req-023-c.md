- NDIS Act 2013, compilation 28 C2026C00451, s 73K: a registration continues in force past its end day while an application for renewal made in the six months before that day is being decided. The path reads this as `applicationInsideWindow` (true from the day the window opens to the period's last day), and the apply step's line, the account page's position sentence and the last-call reminder all read the one function (finding 83).

(finding 46).
