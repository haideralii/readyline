## Instruments (read 16 October 2026 by the third-pass reviewer; the quotes are in the review file)

- NDIS Act 2013, compilation 28 C2026C00451: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2013A00020/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/word. On screen: "Your renewal window opens" is the period end minus 6 months, s 73K(1)(b) "within 6 months before that day", with s 73K(2) "the registration of the person continues in force until the Commissioner makes a decision"; "Your registration period ends" is the certificate's date, s 73E(5)(e); the sentence about an earlier application not keeping the registration in force follows s 73K(1)(b) (finding 83); the three-year record-keeping line beside the pack link is s 73R.
- NDIS (Provider Registration and Practice Standards) Rules 2018, compilation 6 F2026C00527: https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2018L00631/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/word. On screen: "Your mid-term audit must start by" is the period start plus 18 months, s 13B(4)(a), "or (b) such longer period after the beginning of that period as the Commissioner allows", which is the override field with its source (a certificate condition, Act s 73G(3)(b), or the Commissioner's letter); verification providers have no mid-term audit, s 13B(1); the s 13B(7) exemptions are derived from the questionnaire for the two the product models (early childhood as the only certification class; SDA only) and the transitioned-provider exemption is named on the form and not modelled (D-2315).
- The suggestions ("Prepare for your mid-term audit from", start plus 12 months; "Apply for renewal by", period end minus 3 months; the 28-day and 14-day lead times) are the product's and say "our suggestion" on screen (D-2302). One reason for the 3-month lead is the Commission's apply page: "You must complete your application within 60 days of starting it or it will be deleted" (practice; whether it binds a renewal is UNVERIFIED, finding 89).
