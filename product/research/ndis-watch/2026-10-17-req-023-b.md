## What Session B relies on

- R-5 of the REQ (the webinar research of 14 October 2026, practice and not a rule): an auditor at renewal samples documents and registers across the registration period, with weight on the period since the last audit. So the pack and the auditor page default to the whole registration period (D-2305) and "since the last audit" is a filter and a summary, never the pack's default.
- R-1: the period's first and last day come from the certificate (NDIS Act s 73E(5)(e)); the filter reads them from `registration_periods` as typed and never derives them.
- Nothing in the Rules or the Act names "the period since the last audit"; the phrase is the auditor's practice and the product's label, and the screen says what it shows ("Showing: Since the last audit (from <date>)") rather than what an auditor will do (D-2307).
