# NDIS watch, 13 October 2026

Research only. VERIFIED means a Commission or legislation.gov.au source was read this run; UNVERIFIED means the best source was a government department, state body or secondary site, so confirm before relying on it.

## Summary

1. First report, so this is the baseline; the repo's standards data (11 Oct 2026) still matches the Commission's Practice Standards, and the SIL quality indicators are also on legislation.gov.au as F2026N00443.
2. The Provider Registration Rules changed on 1 July 2026 (F2026L00802): notify the Commission of events and changes as soon as you know they will happen, and a change of ownership triggers a certification audit within three months; the primer does not say this yet.
3. The Commission's portals now use myID and RAM, not PRODA, and a new portal launched in October 2026; the primer still says "PRODA login".
4. Worker screening clearances began expiring in February 2026; the Commission's renewal FAQ (90-day renewal window, immediate removal on expiry, database linking) belongs in the worker screening procedure.
5. Mandatory registration expands to personal care, daily living and closed-setting supports from 1 July 2027 (government timeline); a consultation closes 20 October 2026, and the exact classes are so far only in secondary sources.

## Proposed changes to the generator

1. **Primer, "Registration pathway" step 2 (PRODA).** VERIFIED. Finish the application within 60 days of starting it or it is deleted." Remove the `UNVERIFIED` tag. Sources: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/applications-portal-login and https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/apply-registration. The October 2026 launch of the new combined portal is UNVERIFIED (Commission portal pages block fetching; ClinicComply 30 Sep 2026 says 12 October).

2. **Primer step 7 "Ongoing obligations"; `governance-policy` brief.** VERIFIED. F2026L00802 (in force 1 July 2026) inserted s 13A: notice of an event or change in the Commissioner's approved form, by the earlier of becoming aware it will occur and its occurring (for a change of ownership, when aware or when a necessary preliminary step occurs). New s 13BA: a change-of-ownership audit must start within the 3 months after the change, with the report to the Commissioner within 28 days. Primer wording: "Notify the Commission of changes and events in the approved form as soon as you know they will happen, and at the latest when they happen; a change of ownership that significantly changes governance triggers a certification audit within three months." Governance policy "What we do" line: "We tell the Commission about changes and events in its approved form as soon as we know they will happen." Source: https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L00802/asmade/text (compiled Rules F2026C00527).

3. **Primer "Rules the registers must respect", reportable incidents.** VERIFIED. The Incident Rules (F2018L00633, unamended since 2018): s 20 covers death, serious injury, abuse or neglect, unlawful sexual or physical contact and sexual misconduct (24 hours, remainder within 5 business days); s 21 covers unauthorised restrictive practice (5 business days, in writing); s 25 keeps the record seven years from the notification. The primer's seed wrongly puts unauthorised restrictive practice under 24 hours. Replace the bullet with: "Reportable incidents: death, serious injury, abuse or neglect, unlawful sexual or physical contact, and sexual misconduct must be notified within 24 hours (Rules s 20), with the full report within 5 business days; unauthorised use of a restrictive practice within 5 business days in writing (s 21). Keep the record for seven years from the notification (s 25)." Remove the `UNVERIFIED` tag; the register code already matches. Source: https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2018L00633/latest/text.

4. **Fixed text `retention-schedule` (`src/lib/generator/fixed-extras.ts`).** VERIFIED for the incident row; UNVERIFIED for claims. 5. **`worker-screening-and-recruitment-procedure`, `clearance-register` fixed text, primer "Worker screening".** VERIFIED.
