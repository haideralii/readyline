Your audit: verification (an auditor checks your documents at a desk; nobody visits).
15 documents and 5 registers.
A document counts when it is filled in, read, and adopted.
About how long: most documents take 15 to 30 minutes to fill in and read; the self-assessment about an hour.
The owner's name, role and the approval date fill in from your account and on adoption.
If a blank does not apply to you, write "not applicable" and why; do not delete the line.
self-assessment: the form where you say how you meet each rule.
adopt: sign and date a document so it is yours.
You have 5 things to fill in across 3 documents.
1. Governance policy: Who decides what; read first.
2. Risk management policy and register: Every other document points here.
3. Continuity of supports plan: What happens if you cannot.
4. Conflict of interest policy: Auditors ask on day one.
5. Privacy and information management policy: Participant information, the law behind it.
6. Records management policy: Where every record lives.
7. Complaints management policy, procedure and register: A Rules requirement with a register.
8. Incident management policy, procedure and register: A Rules requirement with a register.
9. Emergency and disaster management plan: Required since the 2021 changes.
