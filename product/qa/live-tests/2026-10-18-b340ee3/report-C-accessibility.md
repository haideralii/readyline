# Readyline live QA — report C: accessibility by use

Target: https://readyline-zeta.vercel.app (production, release sha b340ee3), 18 October 2026. Chromium (Playwright 1.64), signed out throughout. No account, no email, no password, nothing submitted except the public questionnaire's steps (sole trader "Sunrise Supports", NSW, registration group 0107, restrictive practices "No", 3 workers, "No" to the five "already have" questions). Sequential requests, about one a second.

Method: the project's own helpers were reused where they exist. 200 % text is emulated the way `e2e/helpers/width.ts` `setTextZoom` does it (`document.documentElement.style.fontSize = "200%"`), the sideways-scroll scan is the `widthReport` logic, the focus-ring test is the `focusInfo` logic from `e2e/helpers/a11y.ts` (outline or box-shadow on the element, or on its label for a visually hidden input), and the reduced-motion scan is `reducedMotionMax`. On top of those: Tab order versus bounding boxes, text clipping, 44 px control heights, overlap of control boxes, contrast of sampled text nodes, and ARIA structure read from the rendered DOM. axe is not repeated here (report B covers it). Scripts: `C-lib.mjs` (shared), `C-keyboard.mjs`, `C-extra.mjs`, `C-zoom.mjs`, `C-media.mjs`, `C-sr.mjs`, evidence scripts `C-focus-theft.mjs`, `C-probe-stall*.mjs`, `C-shots-extra.mjs`; results `C-results-*.json` (`C-results-merged.json` is the set the tables below are built from); screenshots `shots/C-*.png` (93 files).

### Judgement calls (recorded as NOTE, not FAIL)

- **"Jump to" links do not move focus onto the heading.** On `/guides/which-ndis-audit` and `/terms` (1280 aside nav and 360 inline nav) Enter on a Jump to link scrolls the heading to 96 px from the top, sets the hash, and leaves `document.activeElement` on `<body>`; the headings carry no `tabindex="-1"`. Chromium does move the sequential focus start point: the next Tab lands on the first focusable element after the heading, so a keyboard user is not sent back to the top. This is below the brief's "move focus to the heading" and the usual fix is `tabindex="-1"` on the h2s, but it is not a WCAG failure. Rows 42, 60, 98.
- **Escape closes nothing.** The glossary and FAQ disclosures and the result sections are native `<details>` (Escape is not expected); "Show steps" at 360 is a `<details>` rendered as an absolute overlay and stays open on Escape (closes with Enter/Space on its summary; the open list stays inside the viewport at 16,133 328x282); the "Start again" confirm is an inline `role=group`, not a dialog, with "Keep" focused so one Enter dismisses it. None is modal and none traps focus, so none fails WCAG; a dialog-like Escape on the two overlays would be a nicety. Rows 8, 91, 96.
- **The focused step heading has no ring.** On arrival at each step focus is placed on the `h1` (`tabindex="-1"`, `outline: none` via `.focus-quiet`). Deliberate per `globals.css`; the heading is not interactive, so 2.4.7 does not apply. Row 63.
- **Column jump on the guide at 1280.** Tab goes through the article (left column) and then into the sticky aside's Jump to list (right column, higher on the page: from (116, 5698) to (843, 5060)). Two-column document order, not a backwards move. Row 35.
- **Stepper before the heading.** At 360 the "Show steps" control sits before the focused h1 in the DOM, so a keyboard user reaches it with Shift+Tab, not Tab. Row 94.

### What passed, with numbers

- Skip link: first Tab stop on all six public pages, 185–188 x 44 px at (8, 8), opacity 1, 2 px ochre outline; Enter puts focus on `main#main-content` and the next Tab lands inside main.
- Focus rings: every one of 22 / 13 / 17 / 35 / 52 / 22 stops (/, /start, /pricing, guide, registration groups, /terms), every stop through the five questionnaire steps and the 24 on the result showed `outline: solid 2px rgb(180, 70, 31)` (offset 2 px); the sr-only 0107 checkbox rings on its 20x20 label box; the number input adds a box-shadow.
- Tab order: no backwards move on any page; focus wraps body → skip link (no trap); every stop scrolled into view.
- Keyboard questionnaire: Enter on "Start the questionnaire" → step 1 with the h1 focused; arrows move and check radios (sole trader ↔ partnership), Space ticks NSW and 0107, ArrowDown picks "No" / "Yes", Ctrl+A + "3" sets workers, Enter on each "Save and continue" / "See my result" moves on with the next h1 focused; the result arrives with "Your result" focused and the three sections open at lg. Sections, glossary, nested document groups and category buttons (`aria-expanded` tracks the list) toggle with Enter and Space with focus kept.
- Error summary: `role=alert`, `tabindex=-1`, focused on appearance, heading "Please fix the following before continuing", each link targets an existing field (`#tradingName`, `#groups`), `aria-invalid="true"` on the failing fields; the first link moves focus into the field.
- 200 % at 360: 0 elements outside the viewport, 0 clipped text, 0 controls under 44 px, 0 overlaps on /, /start, both guides, /terms, the three steps and the result (except D3/D4).
- 320 at 200 %: h1 at 64 px fits (256 px wide), primary buttons 256 x 112 px, "Save and continue" at the bottom of step 1 clickable and lands on step 2; documents are 34–57 screens long, which is the cost of 400 % and expected.
- Dark: body `rgb(27, 36, 34)` / `rgb(241, 237, 230)` = 13.6:1, primary button `rgb(224, 115, 74)` on ink 5.08:1, no sampled text node under threshold on any of the 10 pages; light body `rgb(247, 243, 236)`. Reduced motion: longest animation/transition 0.00001 s, `scroll-behavior` auto everywhere.
- Structure: `lang`, unique titles, one main, navs named "Account", "Prices", "Questionnaire steps", "Jump to", "Legal and about"; stepper line "Step N of 6: Name", current circle "Step N, Name: current", done circles "Step N, Name: done, change it"; every input labelled, radios and checkboxes inside a fieldset with a legend; the ready-line SVG sits inside an `aria-hidden="true"` wrapper with a text sentence beside it; no nameless or generic links; same-name links go to the same href.
