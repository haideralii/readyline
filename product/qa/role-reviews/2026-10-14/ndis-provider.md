I would close the tab and ask the Facebook group whether anyone has used it. **Severity:** I could not do my job (I would not pay). **Fix:** fill it in or take the sentence out until it can be filled.
2. **Where:** the self-assessment, "Items to prepare before the audit", and the result page. **Seen:** the result said "You will need 15 documents and 5 registers." The document says I must prepare "Your document control register", "Your record of decisions or meeting minutes", "Your corporate risk register", "Your participant risk register", "Your conflict of interest register", "Your delegations register", "Your insurances and vehicles register", "Your improvement register", "Your staff matrix", "A signed and dated onboarding checklist for each worker and each participant", then the incident and complaints registers. The app gives me four registers. **Expected:** the same list in both places, and a place in the app for each one, or a sentence that says "keep these in your own folder". A delegations register for a sole trader is me writing down who acts when I am away; fine, but tell me that. **Severity:** I would get it wrong (I would think I bought the wrong thing, or start twelve spreadsheets). **Fix:** either the registers exist in the app before the document lists them, or the list says which ones are mine to keep on paper and gives me the one-line version of each.
