# Role review 2 of 7: product manager (does the path make sense, does it match the REQs, what is missing or half-built)

- **Environment, SHA, devices, account:** as in `qa.md` (local dev server against the hosted project; `req-010-performance-budget` 5209a5e; desktop and Android journeys; "Sunrise Supports" test tenants; fresh fixture render of the sole-trader pack).
- **Read first:** `qa.md`. I agree with its findings 3, 4, 5 and 8 and say so below where I lean on them; I disagree with the weight QA gives finding 6 (see 5 here).
- **Severity scale:** blocker / major / minor.

## Findings

1. **Where:** `/pricing`. **Seen:** the page opens with "Your document pack", the AUD 399 price and the Buy button, then "What you get", "What it is not", refunds, "How the drafts are made", and only then "Audit-ready subscription. AUD 39 a month for the incident, complaints, worker check and policy review registers". **Expected:** CLAUDE.md v2 section 3 and the Phase 4 note: "the pricing page presents the subscription as the main offer with the pack as the entry step". The strategy says the pack is the wedge and the subscription is the product, and the page says the opposite by its order and its weight. **Severity:** major (the primary metric is pack-to-subscription conversion and the page never frames the pack as a step towards anything). **Fix:** one page in two halves of equal weight, the subscription described first as "what keeps you audit-ready" with the pack as "where you start", the registers' names and the reminder emails named, and one line that says the pack is included in the first month or stays a separate one-off (a Haider decision, D-? to record).
2. **Where:** `/` and `/start/result`. **Seen:** the subscription appears on the home page only as "Optional ongoing registers for incidents, complaints, worker screening expiry and policy reviews" in the "What you get" list, and on the result page as "the registers follow as a subscription". **Expected:** the same framing as 1: every path ends in the subscription, and the result page's "What happens next" should name the price and what the registers do for the mid-term audit or renewal. **Severity:** minor on its own, major together with 1. **Fix:** one sentence on the result page's "What happens next" and a card on the home page after "See exactly what you get".
3. **Where:** the fresh pack, Practice Standards self-assessment, section 5 "Items to prepare before the audit". **Seen:** twelve records "an auditor asks to see, in the order they ask": a document control register, a record of decisions, a corporate risk register, a participant risk register, a conflict of interest register, a delegations register, an insurances and vehicles register, an improvement register, a staff matrix, an onboarding checklist, the incident register and the complaints register. The app on this lineage offers four registers (incidents, complaints, worker checks, policy reviews) and the result told the provider "5 registers". **Expected:** the pack promises what the product can keep, or says plainly which records live outside Readyline. **Why:** batch 42 took the list from REQ-018 (nine new registers) which is on `req-018-audit-day-systems` and has not merged; `req-019-review-fixes` put the list into the self-assessment ahead of it. **Severity:** major (sequencing: a customer who buys from this lineage reads twelve records, finds four in the app, and the result said five). **Fix:** merge order: REQ-018 before or with `req-019-review-fixes`, or gate the twelve-record list on the registers that exist in the deployed app.
4. **Where:** `/path/audit-day`. **Seen:** QA findings 3 and 4 (group order, "Show me: Read your..."). **Expected:** REQ-017 FR-3 promised "what the auditor will ask for, in the order they ask, written as the conversation". **Severity:** major: this is the page a provider prints and takes into the audit, and it is the one REQ-017 screen that does not do what the REQ says. I agree with QA.
5. **Where:** `/start/result`. **Seen:** QA finding 6 (20 versus 28). **My view:** minor, not worth a cycle before the trial; the "28 items" line is useful because the checklist page says the same number. Leave it until a trial provider asks.
6. **Where:** `/` ("Why one price, and who is behind it"). **Seen:** "[Name and ABN to be added when registered.]" **Expected:** the launch plan's pre-launch step 1 (ABN registered, brand cleared) done before anyone sees the page. **Severity:** blocker for a public trial, nothing for a private one. **Fix:** Haider's ABN and business name (owed since batch 10); the sentence renders only once set.
7. **Where:** the document set. **Seen:** the pricing page lists 16 documents and the result says 15 documents plus the self-assessment; the two AI documents the brief (CLAUDE.md v2 section 3, point 3: "the set always includes an AI use and governance policy and a data-handling procedure for AI and digital tools") are retired, and their content is an "Appendix: AI and digital tools" inside the privacy policy and a "Use of digital tools" consent line. **Expected:** either the brief wording is updated to say the AI governance content lives in the privacy policy, or the pricing page says it ("includes your AI and digital tools rules"). Today a buyer who read the brief's promise or the `/ai` page cannot see where it landed. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** one line on `/pricing` and `/ai`; a brief footnote.
8. **Where:** the registers subscription. **Seen:** `/registers` keeps incidents, complaints, worker checks and policy reviews with reminders; `/account` shows "Due soon". **Expected (brief section 3, point 5):** "incident register, complaints register, worker screening expiry tracker, policy review reminders, mid-term audit countdown". The countdown is not in the product and not in any open REQ except as the audit-day page's "You have 4 of 28 things ready". **Severity:** minor now (verification providers have no mid-term audit), major once certification providers are the first audience (brief 3a v2.5). **Fix:** a REQ-018 session or a backlog row: a registration date on the account (the column exists since REQ-009) and a countdown on `/registers` and `/account` for certification providers.
9. **Where:** `/account`. **Seen:** QA finding 5 ("1 entries, all current" beside an open reportable incident). **My view:** major, same as QA: the account page is where a subscriber checks they are in order, and it says they are when they are not.
10. **Where:** the questionnaire and result for the target customer. **Seen:** the path from `/start` to the result takes the sole-trader case through five steps with the result in one card, the audit sentence, the counts and one button. **Expected:** this. Nothing missing on the free path; the completion-rate metric is the thing to instrument (no first-party analytics yet, Phase 6). **Severity:** minor (measurement gap). **Fix:** cookie-less first-party counters on questionnaire start, result, buy, subscribe, with the figures on `/admin`.
11. **Where:** `/documents` while generation is off. **Seen:** the Buy box and, below it, "Document generation is coming soon." **Expected:** when `GENERATION_ENABLED` is off the Buy box should not be shown (nobody should be able to pay for a pack the server cannot draft). **Severity:** minor if production always has the flag; it is an environment the trial will not see. **Fix:** hide the Buy box when generation is disabled, with the "coming soon" line in its place.

## Nothing found in

Price disclosure and refund wording on `/pricing`, `/refunds` and the Buy box (fixed, disclosed, no instalments, no interest, cancellation self-service, matches CLAUDE.md 5); the home page's four trust claims (sourced, hosted in Australia, no participant data, independent) and the sample document's framing ("a real document from a recorded run, not a template"); the questionnaire's scope against the brief; the result page's reasons with sources; the registers' no-participant-data design (codes panel, warnings on every free-text field); the legal footer and trade mark statement; the claims language (no "guaranteed", "approved", "audit-proof" anywhere on the pages or in the pack).
