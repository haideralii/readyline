## Stage 1: desk review, by outcome

### Human resource management (verification module)

- **Indicator: workers competent, qualified, experienced; screening, orientation, training.** Worker screening and recruitment procedure sections 3 to 6; induction and training procedure sections 3 to 6; Code of Conduct acknowledgement signed by "Jane Citizen, Owner, 14 October 2026". Role, time and record on every step ("Owner: We record the clearance details and expiry date in the register. See section 6"). **Grade: C on paper.** Deciding quote: "A worker in a risk-assessed role does not work until their clearance is current." Evidence to close: the owner's own clearance in the worker checks register (empty in the app at audit: "Worker checks: 0 entries. All current."), the orientation module certificate, the training register rows.
- **Observation:** the pack's clearance register template asks for "Clearance type and number" and the app stores no number (domain expert 9). An auditor checks the NDIS Worker Screening Database by the number; a register without it sends the owner to another system at the table. Not an NC against the standard; a design inconsistency the provider would feel.
- **Minor NC risk:** two review cadences for the same register ("checks this register each month" versus "every 3 months", field specialist 7). An auditor asks "which one do you do?", and the answer must match the records.

### Incident management (verification module)

- **Indicator: an incident management system relevant and proportionate; incidents acknowledged, responded to, well-managed, learned from; complies with the Rules.** Policy statement, section 5 steps, section 7 escalation table with the s 20 and s 21 windows, section 8 procedure with role, time and record on every row but one. **Grade: minor NC.** Deciding quote: "Find the cause and decide actions: Owner: As soon as possible after the incident." (no time). A second minor would attach if adopted as is: the table says "with the open-disclosure words in this procedure" and the procedure has no such words (field specialist 1); the policy commits to something the document does not carry.
- **Major NC risk (Rules record content):** the pack's incident register template (section 9) has four columns: Reference, Date received, Summary and actions, Outcome and improvement, with example "[C-001]". The Incident Rules s 12 (heading confirmed today; content UNVERIFIED by the domain expert today, relied on from the primer) require the record to show, among other things, whether the incident was reportable and the notification; the template cannot show it. The app's register can (category, severity, reportable, notified on, status, notify-by). If the provider keeps the paper template and not the app, this is a major; if the app, it is closed. **Grade: major NC for the paper template, C for the app register.** Evidence to close: adopt the app's columns in the template (field specialist 4).
- **Observation:** restrictive practices. The safeguarding policy's fixed position (five kinds, the emergency-use steps, the reporting windows) is the clearest restrictive practice text in a verification pack I have seen; the incident policy's own line "We do not use restrictive practices" is consistent with it. The domain expert's 1 (24 hours "if harmed") is a wording imprecision, conservative in effect; I would ask the owner to explain it, not grade it.

### Complaints management (verification module)

- **Indicator: a complaints management and resolution system; participants know how to complain; complaints welcomed, acknowledged, respected, well-managed; follows the Rules.** Policy sections 4 to 7; a procedure with nine numbered steps and a table of the provider's own time frames (acknowledge within 1 business day, update every 5 business days, outcome within 21 days where possible, close within 2 business days); the fixed "How to contact the Commission" block; the register template with reference codes. **Grade: C on paper, minor NC on adoption.** Deciding quote for the minor: "[Provider to complete: the best phone number and email address for people to contact Sunrise Supports with a complaint]" — until filled, the participant is not told how to complain to the provider (indicator "knowledge of and access to"). Evidence to close: the Finish answer, the service agreement's complaints contact line, one complaint in the register or a dated note that none was received.
- **Observation:** the drafted step quotes an interpreter number no document sources; the fixed block beneath it is sourced (domain expert 2 and 3). I would ask which card the participant is given.

### Risk management (verification module)

- **Indicator: a documented risk management system for WHS, proportionate; emergency and disaster planning; regular risk assessments including participant reliance and disruption; insurance; infection control.** Risk policy sections 4 to 8 (each commitment tagged to a quality indicator), risk areas table, register with one example row; emergency and disaster plan; continuity of supports plan with a backup provider arrangement (blank). **Grade: minor NC.** Deciding quote: the register holds "Example: a worker is unavailable at short notice | Medium | Backup worker list; continuity of supports plan | [Owner] 14 October 2026" and "[Risk] | [Low / Medium / High]"; a register with an example and no live row is a template, not a system. Evidence to close: three to five real rows with review dates; the certificates of currency (the checklist says the owner has them: "You have it" on the three insurance lines).
- **Minor NC risk:** "Participant reliance" is committed to (policy statement, quality indicator 4) but no participant-level record is named for it; the risk register is corporate. The twelve-record list in the self-assessment names "Your participant risk register, one line per participant", which the app on this lineage does not provide. Evidence to close: one line per participant anywhere (a page in the support plan would do).

### Document control and the quality system (all documents)

- Every document: title, provider, version "review-pack", drafted 14 Oct 2026, approver "Jane Citizen, Owner", approval date, next review 14 Oct 2027, a change log with one row. **Grade: C**, with an observation: sixteen documents drafted, approved and change-logged on the same day read as a batch; I would ask what changed between draft and adoption and expect "I read each one" with the Adopt dates in the app.
- **Placeholders:** the fresh render carries 2 to 5 brackets in most documents, 10 in the records policy and 15 in the service agreement template (QA's count). The service agreement's are per-participant fields and are right; the records policy's ten retention blanks and the three "In plain words: Generic text: add your details." fallbacks are not. Three or more instructional placeholders across a pack is a major for the quality management system by my rule. **Grade: major NC on the pack as rendered today; closed by the Finish step for the business facts and by the recording session for the three summaries; still open for the retention periods unless the product supplies them.**
- **Consistency of names:** the app's "Worker checks" page versus the pack's "Worker screening clearance register" (batch 42 made the pack say "Worker checks register" in fixed text, but the recorded worker screening procedure still says "worker screening clearance register" in six places); "Training register" exists in the pack and not in the app. **Grade: observation** (a provider asked "show me your worker checks register" would know; "clearance register" would make them hesitate).
- **Cross-references:** every "see section N" I followed in the four verification documents landed on the right heading. **C.**

## Stage 2: the evidence trail (registers and app)

- **Participant, service agreement to support plan review:** the template exists; no participant record exists in the app by design (no participant data). An auditor samples the paper files; nothing to grade here, but the checklist should carry "a signed service agreement for each participant" (it does not; the onboarding checklist in the twelve-record list would).
- **Worker, screening to supervision to appraisal:** the worker checks register is empty for the owner; the induction procedure sets supervision for new workers only; no appraisal record is named anywhere. **Observation** for a sole trader; a minor NC the day the first worker starts.
- **Incident, report to notification to improvement:** INC-0001 (restrictive practice used without authorisation, low, reportable, 9 Oct, notify by 16 Oct) with a trail entry "Recorded" and "Add a note". The notify-by date is right for s 21 (five business days from the day reported). What is missing on the row: a "notified on" date field appears only on edit, and there is no link from the incident to an improvement; the pack's escalation table promises "The improvement register, within 5 business days of closing the incident" and the app has no improvement register on this lineage. **Minor NC** on the improvement loop (the policy commits to a record that does not exist here).
- **Complaint, receipt to resolution to improvement:** no row; the register's statuses (acknowledged, resolved) exist; the same improvement gap.
- **Policy, adoption to circulation to review:** the Adopt step records adoption; "Policy reviews: 0 entries. All current." before anything is adopted is accurate for the test account; the reminder rules are unit-tested (not re-run here). The circulation to workers is "All workers: read the current version at induction and after each change" in every document's review table, with no record named. **Observation** for a sole trader; minor NC with workers.
- **Reminders that would fire late:** the incident's notify-by is shown on the row the moment it is saved, and the account page's "Due soon" reads "Nothing due in the next 30 days" while that date is two days away (QA 5, provider 9). **Minor NC** (the reminder surface contradicts the register).
