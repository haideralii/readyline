149. FR-4 (ii), FR-5, Data, AC-2 and D-2412: "the worker holds a check that state's s 14A(2) paragraph names, recorded as a `worker_clearances` row of the new kind `acceptable-state-check`", and AC-2 "given the same with an `acceptable-state-check` row for NSW, then the option is offered". Read today, s 14A(2) names, for NSW, SA and WA, "an acceptable NSW check", "an acceptable SA check" and "an acceptable WA check", and for Victoria, Queensland, Tasmania, the ACT and the NT not a check at all but that "the person is allowed to engage in a risk assessed role because of subsection 25(3)" (26(3), 27(3), 28(3), 29(3)), each "or an acceptable aged care check". Every one of those is a Part 4 transitional arrangement with its own end date: s 23(5) to (7), an acceptable NSW check is a criminal record check obtained during the transition period (which ended on 31 January 2021 at the latest) and no more than two years old, or a NSW WWCC "issued ... no later than the last day of the transition period" (a five-year card, so expired by 31 January 2026); s 24(6) to (6B), an acceptable SA check is a finding made before or within two months after the end of the SA transition period (31 January 2021) and "not more than 3 years since the finding" (expired by April 2024); s 29A(5), an acceptable WA check exists "at a time ... before 1 February 2023"; ss 25(3), 26(3), 27(3), 28(3) and 29(3) each apply only "during the transition period" (ended 31 January 2021, NT 30 June 2021). Only the aged care provider check (s 29B(5): an approved residential aged care provider's staff member or volunteer with a police certificate within three years) can be held on 16 October 2026, and only by a provider that is itself an approved aged care provider serving a resident of its own facility (s 29B(1)). So the escape v6 builds cannot lawfully be open for any new worker today; a row of the new kind would open the pending exception for a worker who applied in a "no card, no start" state and serves a NSW participant, on a check the Rules say expired. Fix: drop `acceptable-state-check` and the FR-4 (ii) escape; the national block's `acceptableChecks` records, per state served, the Part 4 section and the date its arrangement ended, so the gate reads "no acceptable check applies after <date>" with the source; the one live case is recorded as the owner's own-call code (1)(b) with a help text naming s 29B (as the three ungated exceptions already are, FR-6a); AC-2's two acceptable-check cases become "the option is absent and the line says the state arrangement ended on <date>".
