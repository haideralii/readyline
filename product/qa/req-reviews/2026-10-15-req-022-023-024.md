**2. Conflicts**

**3. Domain claims**

## REQ-023 Renewal readiness

**1. Testability**
1. AC-1 "the position sentence for today": inject a fixed clock and state the expected sentence (e.g. today 15 Oct 2026 -> "You are 19 months in").
2. AC-5 "every step shows its live state" is unverifiable; list the expected state per step for the certification fixture.
3. FR-4 (checklist items), FR-9 (verification wording), FR-11's "no automatic rows" and SR-3's parameter validation have no AC.

**2. Conflicts**
4. FR-2 mid-term window "15 to 21 months" contradicts the primer: the mid-term audit "must start no later than 18 months into the registration period (Rules F2026C00527 s 13B, read 13 Oct 2026)" (`product/domain/ndis-registration-primer.md:20`) and the code (`MID_TERM_DUE_MONTHS = 18`, `src/lib/registers/reminders.ts:34`). AC-1's "1 June 2027 to 1 December 2027" would tell a provider a date after the legal deadline. Rewrite: window 12 to 18 months, "must start by".
5. "Today the product... has no idea of time": `reminders.ts:182-207` already sends `mid-term-audit` (12, 15, 17 months) and `renewal` (6, 3 months before) reminders. FR-3 must say these are replaced, and Data must add the new `reminders.kind` values (CHECK at `20261014200000_audit_day_reminders.sql:21`). Also: editing the expiry (FR-1) leaves already-inserted `reminders` rows keyed on the old `send_on`; say unsent rows for the account's registration kinds are deleted on edit.
6. Data: "the registration row REQ-018 added": it is `accounts.registration_date`/`registration_kind` from REQ-009 (`20261013000000_registers.sql:137`).
7. FR-5 default "Since the last audit" in the audit pack contradicts R-6 (auditor samples "across the registration period"): hiding pre-audit open incidents from the pack by default is an audit risk. Default the pack to "This registration period"; keep since-last-audit as the summary (FR-6).
8. Sessions B, C, D and AC-7 require REQ-020 FR-1, FR-2, FR-4, FR-8, FR-9 (not built). D-2304 must say "after REQ-020".

**3. Domain claims**
9. R-3 (audit booked and finished *before* expiry, application last, FR-7 step order, FR-2's "audit done by" date): I believe the Commission's process is apply for renewal *first* (via the portal, which issues the scope), then audit, and under the NDIS Act the registration continues until the renewal application is decided if lodged before expiry. If so, FR-2's "audit done by expiry - 6 weeks" and FR-7's "application submitted" as the last step are backwards. Uncertain; it is marked VERIFY but the design should not be fixed until read.
10. FR-10 "notify-by date derived from the Rules' timing for that type": the repo's own reading says s 13/13A timing is "as soon as practicable" / "the earlier of" (`data/watch-changes.json:159`) and the primer says "at the latest when they happen". There is no day count to derive. Rewrite: `notify_by = happened_on`; state `overdue` when `notified_on` is null and `happened_on < today`.
11. FR-11/FR-10 treat "registration groups" as a s 13 change. A change of registration groups is a variation of registration (an application), not a notification; the s 13 list in `watch-changes.json:158` is contact details, area, locations, participant and worker numbers. Separate "apply to vary" from "notify".
12. FR-10 channel "phone": notifications are "in the approved form" (primer line 21); drop phone or label it "other".

**4. Privacy and security**
13. SR-1 "the same CHECK as the clearance number" is `^[A-Za-z0-9 /-]*$` <=40 (`20261015010000_clearance_number.sql`), and the "participant-shape" CHECK is only the 43xxxxxxx regex (`looks_like_ndis_number`). It does not catch names; say so rather than "never a participant shape".
14. SR-3 custom range "within the registration period" excludes a renewed provider's previous period; allow any range <= 10 years, validated as two ISO dates, `from <= to`.

**5. Scope and cost**
15. FR-7 "a changed answer set shows which documents it would change" is a document-diff feature the Scope section itself puts out; cut it to a link to the questionnaire.
16. FR-5 on "every register index and detail list" (~15 registers) plus three-project e2e per page is CI-expensive under D-2207; AC-3 covers one register; keep that.
17. Missing for a real provider: the annual declaration date (primer: ongoing obligation, UNVERIFIED) belongs on the timeline more than "book by".

**6. Clarity**
18. AC-2 "on the day each date is reached": the job runs 20:00 UTC; say "on or after".
19. FR-1 "defaults to the granted date for a first registration" vs R-6 "since the last audit": for a first registration the filter is then identical to "This registration period"; say so in the UI.

**Verdict:** Not ready. First three: (4) fix the mid-term window to the 18-month deadline; (10/11) rewrite the changes register around "notify at the latest when it happens" and split variations from notifications; (9) read the renewal page before fixing FR-2/FR-7's order and add the REQ-020 dependency.
