Worker Screening Rules s 14(1)(a) (read 16 Oct 2026) allows a person in a risk-assessed role without a clearance only if "all of the following apply: (i) the person is in the process of obtaining a clearance; (ii) the person is appropriately supervised by a person with a clearance; (iii) the registered NDIS provider is implementing a risk management plan developed and maintained in accordance with Division 3; (iv) the law of the participating jurisdiction ... allows the person to engage in a risk assessed role while that person is in the process of obtaining a clearance"; s 14A(1): where "the law of the participating jurisdiction ... prohibits a person from engaging in a risk assessed role while the person is in the process of obtaining a clearance" the exception does not apply at all unless an "acceptable check" of that jurisdiction applies; and s 15(2)(f) and (g): a person is not "in the process" at any time they are unsupervised or the risk management plan is not being implemented.
