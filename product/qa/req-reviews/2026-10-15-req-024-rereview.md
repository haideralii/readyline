## 3. Domain claims

The REQ's W-1, W-1a and W-1b match the Word copy of F2021C00788 word for word where they quote (s 13(a) and (c), s 14(1)(a)(i) to (iv), s 17(2) and (3), s 18(3), s 20, s 21). The findings are what the quotes leave out.

12. W-1a: "the law of the jurisdiction allows a person to work in a risk assessed role while the application is determined (VERIFY per state)". The Rules say how that works, and the REQ does not name it: s 14A(1), "Paragraphs 14(1)(a) and 14(2)(a) do not apply to a person if: (a) the person has submitted an application for a clearance to an NDIS worker screening unit; and (b) the law of the participating jurisdiction in which the application is submitted prohibits a person from engaging in a risk assessed role while the person is in the process of obtaining a clearance; and (c) the person does not have an acceptable check that applies in that participating jurisdiction; and (d) a decision has not been made on the application"; s 14A(2) lists the acceptable checks per state (an acceptable NSW check, SA check or WA check, or an acceptable aged care check; Victoria, Queensland, Tasmania, the ACT and the NT through ss 25(3) to 29(3) of Part 4). Fix: W-1a cites s 14A and the per-state VERIFY says what is to be read (whether the state is "no card, no start" and which acceptable checks it recognises), so the fourth tick line can carry that state's wording.
13. FR-4: the first tick line "application lodged on <date>". Under s 15(3) a person has submitted a complete application, and so is "in the process of obtaining a clearance" (s 15(1)), only when "(a) that unit has issued a notice in writing to the person confirming that the application has been made; and (aa) a registered NDIS provider has confirmed to that unit that the person is, or intends to be, a worker ...; and (b) ... the registered NDIS provider ... has seen that notice; and (c) ... the registered NDIS provider has made a record of the worker screening application number on that notice as required by section 18"; and under s 15(2)(f) and (g) the person stops being in the process "at any time the person is not being supervised in accordance with the requirements of section 14" or "a risk management plan required under this instrument is not being implemented". Fix: the first line reads "the screening unit's written notice seen on <date>; application number <number> recorded; we confirmed to the unit that this person is our worker", the application number is required (not optional) to start the exception, and the end date is also set when supervision or the plan stops.
14. W-1a (iii) and FR-4's third tick line, "our risk management plan is in place and followed". Division 3 (ss 11 and 12) requires a written plan; nothing in the catalogue is that plan: `data/readyline-content/document-types.json` has the worker screening and recruitment procedure (lines 673-695, with a clearance register section) and no document or section for a risk management plan for workers pending clearance (no match for "risk management plan" or "Division 3"). Fix: name the document or section that is the Division 3 plan (a section in the worker screening procedure, generated with the account's facts, or a new document type), or the tick line says "attach your Division 3 risk management plan" and the item is not done until a date is typed against it.
15. W-4: "VERIFY the current name and whether the Commission states it as a requirement or an expectation". The Commission's pages were unreachable (UNVERIFIED: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/providers/worker-screening and https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/workforce/workforce-capability, three timeouts at 60 s on 15 Oct 2026). The repository already holds the Commission's own words in the Practice Standards quality indicators, dataset 1.2.0, verified 2026-10-11: `data/standards.json:973`, core governance, human resource management, "An orientation and induction process is in place that is completed by workers including completion of the mandatory NDIS worker orientation program", and `:3214` for the verification module, "Workers complete mandatory NDIS orientation module". Fix: W-4 cites those two indicators as the source for "mandatory", keeps VERIFY only for the module's current title ("Quality, Safety and You" is the primer's name, line 48), and the item's source line quotes the indicator.
16. FR-5: "first aid and CPR with defaults 3 years and 1 year as constants". No Rule or Practice Standard sets those periods; they are training-sector practice (the first-aid unit's refresher guidance), and REQ-018 FR-8 handled the same thing by calling its cadences "Readyline's suggestion". Fix: the expiry typed from the certificate wins, the defaults are "Readyline's suggestion", one constant each with a comment (the D-2302 pattern), and the item's ask says "check the date on the certificate".
17. FR-6a: "any suspension or revocation as a code and date". s 18(3)(d)(iv) and (v) ask for "whether the worker's clearance is subject to a decision to suspend or revoke a clearance, or any other decision which has the effect that the registered NDIS provider may not allow the worker to engage in a risk assessed role; and (v) the nature of any decision mentioned in subparagraph (iv)", and s 15(2)(e) names the interim bar. Fix: the closed list is interim bar, suspension, revocation, other decision, each with a date and a capped note for its nature.
18. FR-1: "contractor" in the role list. The Rules' "worker" is "an individual employed or otherwise engaged by a registered NDIS provider" (s 5), so a contractor engaged directly is a worker; an individual supplied by a labour-hire "personnel provider" is "personnel", allowed into a risk assessed role only under s 13(b) (role identified, an "appropriate contract" under s 5A, reasonable steps to check the clearance) with the s 19 records, none of which the product holds. Fix: the role reads "contractor (engaged by you)" and the item's source line says labour-hire personnel need the s 13(b) contract and s 19 records, out of scope.

## 4. Privacy and security

20. Data: "Retention: 7 years from the record's date (s 21); the purge job (REQ-010) learns the rule; past workers stay in the s 18 export for seven years after `ended_on`". The purge job deletes an account's data 30 days after soft delete (`src/app/api/jobs/purge/route.ts:2`, REQ-004 FR-6) and the privacy policy promises it: "If you delete your account, we remove your documents, answers and notes within 30 days" (`product/legal/privacy.md:47`), as CLAUDE.md 4.5 requires. The seven-year duty is the provider's (s 21), and SR-8(f) says "Readyline does not keep a copy". A product that kept worker rows for seven years after the owner deleted the account would hold personal information of people who are not its customers against the owner's deletion. Fix: retention means no automatic purge of worker rows while the account is open and past workers kept in the export for seven years after `ended_on`; account deletion is unchanged; the privacy policy and the export's first line say the owner's downloaded file is the record that survives.
21. FR-6b and SR-7: "a POST whose body is used to render one response", and SR-8(d) "the server action's argument logging". A Next.js server action returns serialised values to the client, not a streamed file download; the fill has to be a route handler taking `multipart/form-data` with the origin check, the member gate, the subscription gate, `Content-Disposition: attachment` and `Cache-Control: no-store`. Fix: say route handler, name the path (`/registers/staff/export/screening-list` POST), and add the input caps (one row per current worker code, full name 120, date of birth an ISO date, address 200, assessor and supervisor names 120, each refused by `looks_like_ndis_number`), so SR-8(d)'s redaction (`redact.ts` key lists gain `full_name`, `date_of_birth`, `address`, `assessor_name`, `supervisor_name`) has defined keys to match.
22. SR-1: "The privacy policy's 'What we collect' gains the worker lines". Today's "What we collect" (`product/legal/privacy.md:14-19`) lists the questionnaire answers and nothing about workers, and the invitation already stores the worker's email hash on `origin/req-020-team` (REQ-020 Session B, "address stored as a hash"). Fix: the policy diff is Session A's deliverable (Session A exports check numbers), and it names the email hash, the code, the role, the check and application numbers, the dates, and the fill fields as "used for one file, not kept".
23. FR-6 and D-2407: "the auditor link shows codes and states only, no numbers". REQ-020 Session A serves "the signed-out page and its pack" behind the link (NEXT.md line 1638; `src/lib/registers/audit-pack.ts` builds one zip), so the same `workers/` folder and `screening-list.csv` would reach the auditor with the numbers unless the pack is built twice. Fix: FR-6 names two builds from one builder with an audience parameter: the owner's pack with numbers; the link's pack with the number columns dropped and the two Rules lists' number columns removed, never a fill (SR-8(a)), with a test that the link's zip contains no digit-bearing check column.
