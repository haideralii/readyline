# REQ-028: Desktop layout and the design system

Status: draft (PO, 17 Oct 2026 02:40; written from the UI/UX designer and researcher audit of 17 Oct 2026, `product/qa/role-reviews/2026-10-17/ux-audit.md` (7450511, findings 1 to 68, the desktop layout proposal, the colour and type system, the top-10 plan) and the QA pass of the same day, `qa-pass.md` (ac72e9b, F3, F8, F10, F11, N3, N7, N8); goes to the `req-review`, the lawyer role and the NDIS expert (D-2222) before "ready"; nothing starts until then. Haider's ask of 17 Oct 2026: "it should be better for desktop as well, because zip files will be downloaded on laptops not phones, so we need to cater for both correctly".)
Phase: 6 Priority: should Owner: Dev

## Why

The product was built phone-first, which was right for the questionnaire and the result: a sole trader meets Readyline on a phone. The paid half of the product is laptop work: the documents page and its Word files, the registers, the audit-pack zip, the checklist on the night before the audit, and the long reading pages. Today every page is one 624 px column (`max-w-2xl` in 39 places) under a 1024 px header, nothing changes between 1024 and 1536 px, and the home page's column sits left while every other page's sits centred, so a laptop shows a wide phone with the right half empty and the axis jumping on the first click (audit findings 1, 2, 8). The audit also measured the things a design system should have fixed once: dark mode is near-black with borders at 1.4 to 1.7:1 and helper text that fails AA in seven places (28, 29), inputs have no visible boundary in dark mode (11), the brand's ochre never reaches a radio or a checkbox (10), the ready line, the identity's motif, is invisible at 0 done (34), and three recipes exist for tags, disclosures and secondary buttons (42, 43, 45). This REQ gives the product one shell, one grid, one set of tokens that pass contrast in both schemes, and the laptop layouts for the pages laptops are for, without moving a word of copy that REQ-027 settled.

## Scope

- In: a shared shell container and a 12-column grid from `lg` (1024 px); four new colour tokens and a lifted dark palette applied by search-and-replace to the zinc utilities; form-control basics (accent colour, focus ring, hover, checked state, error clearing); the laptop layouts for the questionnaire, the result page, the documents pages, the registers index and the register pages, the audit pack's button and contents, the checklist, the guides and legal pages, the account page, the home page and pricing; register tables from `sm`; empty states for the registers; one `Tag`, one `Disclosure`, one secondary button; the type scale and the optical-size fix; the stepper's accessible names (QA F3); the 404 title is already in `qa-pass-fixes`.
- Out: any change to copy that REQ-027 D-2709 to D-2730 settled (the meanings, the hint sentences, the result page's lines), the legal pages' words (REQ-022 Session E), the home page's price words (D-2001, D-2265); the phone order of the result page (D-2708, D-2701) and the 360 × 740 fold rule (REQ-015 FR-4), which stand; a theme toggle (the `data-theme` blocks stay as they are until one is wanted, audit 33); the documents' Word rendering; the generated documents' own typography (house-style.md, REQ-016).

## Domain rules that apply

- CLAUDE.md 4.1 to 4.5 and 5: no urgency, no dark patterns, independence from the Commission, honest prices; nothing in a layout may make the subscription look like the pack's price or hide the refund line (D-2001 order stands: the subscription first as "what keeps you audit-ready", the pack second as "where you start", now as two equal cells).
- REQ-014 (visual identity): the tokens, the serif for headings, the ready line as the motif with the "to prepare" segment in ochre, which REQ-014 specified and the build left out (audit 34).
- REQ-015 FR-4 and D-2707: the result card's primary button in the first screen at 360 × 740; one primary action per screen; focus ring 2 px ochre with a 2 px offset on every focusable element (`brand.md`).
- WCAG 2.2 AA: 1.4.3 text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 for large), 1.4.11 non-text contrast 3:1 for the boundary of any control, 2.4.2 page titles, 1.3.1 names on controls. The audit's measured ratios are the record (`ux-audit.md` section 4); the Dev re-measures on delivery with the same formula.
- Worker Screening Rules s 20 (the list "in a form which would allow an auditor to determine" who was engaged on any day) and the registers' purpose: a register table's columns are the fields an auditor samples (date, what, status, due), never a name, a date of birth or an address (REQ-024 D-2407); the NDIS expert's read names the columns per register.

## Functional requirements

- FR-1 The shell. One container, `mx-auto w-full max-w-6xl px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8` (1152 px content at 1216 px and above), used by the site header, the next-step strip, `main` and the footer, so the wordmark, the h1 and the footer links share a left edge and "Sign in" sits above the content's right edge. Under `lg` nothing changes. Inside the shell, from `lg`, `grid grid-cols-12 gap-x-8`. Three page types: reading (main `col-span-8` at `max-w-prose` 65ch, aside `col-span-4 col-start-9` sticky at `top-24`), form (form `col-span-7`, help aside `col-span-4 col-start-9` sticky), list (`col-span-12` with a page-header row holding the h1 left and the primary action right). Every page names its type in one place (a `PageShell` component with a `kind` prop), so no page carries its own `max-w-*` (audit 1, 2, 7, 8). The home page's sections use the shell like every other page (audit 2).
- FR-2 The tokens. `globals.css` gains `--surface`, `--rule`, `--line`, `--track`, `--signal-wash` and `--ochre-wash` in both schemes with the values in D-2802; dark `--paper` and `--mist` are lifted to the D-2802 values; `text-slate` becomes the one helper-text colour; `border-rule` replaces every decorative `border-zinc-200/300` and `dark:border-zinc-800/700`; `border-line` replaces the border of every control (inputs, choice rows, secondary buttons, chips); `bg-surface` replaces `bg-white` (8 uses) and `text-paper` replaces `text-white`; `text-zinc-500` without a dark pair is gone (audit 11, 28 to 32, 35, 66; QA F2's class). A lint in `tests/copy-lint.test.ts`'s walker fails any new `text-zinc-*`, `bg-white`, `text-white` or `dark:border-zinc-*` in `src/` after this REQ lands, with an allow-list of zero.
- FR-3 Form controls. `accent-color: var(--ochre)` on radios and checkboxes; the per-input ink focus classes go and the global ochre `:focus-visible` ring applies; choice rows `hover:bg-mist` and `has-[:checked]:bg-sky has-[:checked]:border-ink`; inputs `bg-surface border-line`; a field's error and its summary entry clear when the field validates on change, and `aria-invalid` with it (audit 9, 10, 12, 13, 14; QA F8). The stepper's circles name themselves with sr-only text, not `aria-label` on a `span` (QA F3), the labels are 14 px, the done circle is `text-paper`, the current circle `bg-paper ring-2 ring-ochre` (audit 15, 16).
- FR-4 The ready line. `ready-line.tsx` draws three segments: done in moss, to prepare in ochre, the remainder in `--track`; height 8 px; the legend names the three; a 0-of-N result shows a full ochre line labelled "N to prepare" (audit 26, 34; REQ-014's spec).
- FR-5 The questionnaire on a laptop. Form type. The h1 row carries "Step N of 6" right in slate; the circle stepper sits under the h1 across the form columns. The aside holds, in order: the "Not sure?" offer (supports; D-2730's sentence unchanged) or the three plain examples (practices) or the sole-trader prefill note (workforce); "What these words mean" for the step, open by default on `lg`; on the supports step "Ticked so far (N)" with remove buttons and the class count by audit type; the draft status and "Start again". The supports groups' class rows are a 2-column grid from `lg`. `Back` and `Save and continue` sit together at the right under the form. Phones keep today's order except that the hint collapses to one line until tapped and the question precedes the examples on the practices step (audit 3, 18 to 22, 49, 50, 62, 63, 65). The glossary disclosure's count is computed from the step's data, not the open DOM, so it is the same at every width (audit 18; D-2721 amended).

## Decisions

- D-2801 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) Desktop is a first-class surface, not a wide phone: one shell at 1152 px, a 12-column grid from `lg`, three page types named on the page. The phone layouts under `lg` stand as REQ-015 and REQ-027 left them; a laptop change never moves a phone element. Haider's reason is the record: the Word files, the registers and the audit pack are downloaded on laptops.
- D-2802 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) The tokens, from the audit's section 4, light / dark: paper #F7F3EC / #1B2422; mist #E6E0D6 / #27322F; ink #1C2B2A / #F1EDE6; slate #4A5553 / #B8C0BD; ochre #B4461F / #E0734A; moss #2F6B4F / #6FBF95; signal #9A2B2B / #E07A7A; sky #DCE8E6 / #1E3330; new: surface #FFFDF9 / #222D2A, rule #D9D2C6 / #34403D, line #7E8785 / #6B7673, track #CFC7B9 / #3A4442, signal-wash #F3E4E2 / #33201F, ochre-wash #F6E6DC / #3A2A22. Rules: ink for body, slate for helper, ochre for the one accent per screen and only on paper or surface (ink on mist), signal for errors, moss for done; `--rule` for decoration, `--line` for anything a person operates; paper → surface → mist → sky as the surface ladder; no `text-zinc-*`, `bg-white` or `text-white` in `src/` after Session A, enforced by the lint. The Dev re-measures every pair on delivery and the table in the evidence is the record; a pair that misses its floor is changed by the Dev to the nearest value that meets it and recorded in D-2802's amendment.
- D-2803 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) The ready line draws done in moss, to prepare in ochre and the remainder in `--track`, as REQ-014 specified; a 0-done result shows the full ochre segment. "To prepare" is the word; the NDIS expert's read confirms it is not a word the Commission uses for a status.
- D-2804 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) The register tables' columns are named per register by the NDIS expert's read before Session B (the fields an auditor samples), never a name, a date of birth, an address or a free-text note column; the note stays on the entry's own page. A column that would put free text in a scannable grid is refused.
- D-2805 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) The result card carries one primary button and one text link (D-2707's rule, restored after REQ-025 and REQ-027 let it grow to three and a sentence): "Sign in to get ready" / "Open your guided path" and "See the price (AUD 399)"; the checklist link lives in the "Documents you will need" header. - D-2806 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) Home keeps D-2001's order and D-2265's words; the layout makes the two prices two equal 16 px cells with tabular figures, the subscription first, each cell linking to its pricing card, the refund sentence inside the pack cell. No bundling, no discount, no urgency word.
- D-2807 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) Errors clear on change, per field, once the field validates; the summary keeps the fields that still fail; the next submit rebuilds it. This is the GOV.UK pattern and the audit's finding 9 and the QA pass's F8.
- D-2808 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) A theme toggle is out; the `data-theme` token blocks stay, the `dark:` variant stays on `prefers-color-scheme`, and `color-scheme` is set from the same token block so native controls follow (audit 33, 68 noted, not built).
- D-2809 (PO, 17 Oct 2026) Session order A, B, C; A is the measuring stick and lands alone, so B and C diff against the system; the hallway test and the VoiceOver note wait for C.
