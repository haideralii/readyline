## Why

The product was built phone-first, which was right for the questionnaire and the result: a sole trader meets Readyline on a phone. The paid half of the product is laptop work: the documents page and its Word files, the registers, the audit-pack zip, the checklist on the night before the audit, and the long reading pages. Today every page is one 624 px column (`max-w-2xl` in 39 places) under a 1024 px header, nothing changes between 1024 and 1536 px, and the home page's column sits left while every other page's sits centred, so a laptop shows a wide phone with the right half empty and the axis jumping on the first click (audit findings 1, 2, 8). The audit also measured the things a design system should have fixed once: dark mode is near-black with borders at 1.4 to 1.7:1 and helper text that fails AA in seven places (28, 29), inputs have no visible boundary in dark mode (11), the brand's ochre never reaches a radio or a checkbox (10), the ready line, the identity's motif, is invisible at 0 done (34), and three recipes exist for tags, disclosures and secondary buttons (42, 43, 45). This REQ gives the product one shell, one grid, one set of tokens that pass contrast in both schemes, and the laptop layouts for the pages laptops are for, without moving a word of copy that REQ-027 settled.
