# REQ-024: Worker onboarding: the worker file an auditor samples, from invitation to first shift

Status: draft, third version (PO, 15 Oct 2026 23:10; the second version's re-review of 15 Oct, `product/qa/req-reviews/2026-10-15-req-024-rereview.md`, 30 findings, every one answered below; Worker Screening Rules F2021C00788 verified by the reviewer as the latest compilation; goes back to the reviewer once more as D-2215 requires; every session waits for REQ-020 to be on `main`)
Phase: 6 Priority: should Owner: Dev

## Why (user and business outcome)

An auditor's worker sample is the fastest way to a non-conformity: "show me three worker files" and the newest worker's file is missing the screening check, the Code of Conduct acknowledgement, the induction or the orientation module, because nobody wrote down what a new worker needs before the first shift. Today the product holds a staff matrix and a per-worker onboarding checklist (REQ-018 FR-9), worker clearances with reminders (REQ-018), invitations and a worker role (REQ-020 FR-4), but they are separate screens with no sequence and the worker cannot do their own part. This REQ extends what exists into one sequence per worker that the owner and the worker tick together, with the dates and numbers an auditor samples, the two lists the Worker Screening Rules require (as far as the product can hold them, D-2406 and D-2408), and one export per worker. It is the third reason to keep the AUD 39 subscription.

## Scope (in / out)

In, as three sessions, all after REQ-020 is on `main`:

- Session A, the onboarding sequence: `staff_matrix`, `onboarding_checklists` and `worker_clearances` extended (not replaced), one new `onboarding_items` table for the mutable state, the owner's views, the worker file export, the two Rules lists with the one-time fill, the privacy policy diff.
- Session B, the worker's own steps through the worker role: the worker completes what is theirs and the owner confirms; the acknowledgement rows; the invitation's first steps.
- Session C, keeping it current: the audit-day and checklist states, the supervision rows, the period filter and the pack (after REQ-023 Session B), ending a worker, the pack's two audiences.

Out: payroll, rostering, contracts, award interpretation, HR integrations; identity documents or certificates as files; a worker's performance notes; anything that names a participant; storing worker names, dates of birth and addresses (D-2406); labour-hire personnel (the s 13(b) contract and the s 19 records, review finding 18); the fill-at-download of every generated document, which is its own REQ after REQ-026 (D-2409).

## Domain rules that apply (link to product/domain)

The Worker Screening Rules, F2021C00788 (the latest compilation, verified by the reviewer 15 Oct 2026):

- W-1 Clearance. A provider in a participating jurisdiction "must only allow a worker to engage in a risk assessed role, if the worker has a clearance" (s 13(a)); a sole-trader provider must hold one themselves (s 13(c)). A clearance lasts up to five years (the primer); the expiry is typed from the outcome, never derived. The provider verifies in the NDIS Worker Screening Database and records the date.
- W-1a The pending exception, s 14(1)(a), all four together: the person "is in the process of obtaining a clearance"; "is appropriately supervised by a person with a clearance"; the provider "is implementing a risk management plan developed and maintained in accordance with Division 3"; and the law of the jurisdiction allows it. "In the process" is defined by s 15: it starts only when the screening unit has issued a written notice confirming the application, the provider has confirmed to the unit that the person is or will be its worker, the provider has seen the notice and has recorded the application number as s 18 requires (s 15(3)); it ends when supervision stops or the plan is not implemented (s 15(2)(f) and (g)), on an interim bar (s 15(2)(e)), or on the decision. Section 14A switches the exception off where the state's law prohibits work in a risk assessed role while the application is pending and the person holds no acceptable check in that state (s 14A(1); the acceptable checks per state in s 14A(2) and Part 4). A sole trader's own pending clearance falls under s 14(2)(a), which needs supervision by a person with a clearance. The work-experience exception (s 14(1)(c)) is out of scope.
- W-1b The Division 3 plan: ss 11 and 12 require a written list of risk assessed roles and a written risk management plan for people with disability while a worker is engaged in a risk assessed role and in the process of obtaining a clearance, reviewed after a reportable incident involving a worker. No document in the catalogue is that plan today (review finding 14): this REQ adds it as a section of the worker screening and recruitment procedure (FR-4a).
- W-1c The records, Part 3: a written list of risk assessed roles with the title, the paragraph of the definition that applies, a description, the date assessed and "the name and title of the person who assessed the role" (s 17(3)), updated within 20 business days of a new or reclassified role (s 17(2)); a written list of each worker in a risk assessed role with "the worker's full name, date of birth and address", the roles, for an exception the section relied on, its start and end dates and the supervisor's name, and otherwise the application number, the check number, the outcome and its expiry, and whether the clearance is subject to a decision to suspend or revoke it or any other decision that stops the worker, and the nature of that decision (s 18(3)); kept in a form that lets an auditor "determine which workers were engaged in risk assessed roles with the provider on any given day in the seven years prior" (s 20); every record in the Part kept 7 years from the date it is made (s 21). The seven-year duty is the provider's; the product's part is D-2406 and the retention rule in Data.
- W-2 Working with children check, where the provider delivers supports to children or the state requires it: a state scheme. VERIFY per state (D-2223 item 2), read into `data/state-screening.json` (FR-2a).
- W-3 NDIS Code of Conduct: every worker is bound by it; the acknowledgement document in the pack (REQ-005) is the record. Source: NDIS (Code of Conduct) Rules 2018.
- W-4 NDIS Worker Orientation Module. The Practice Standards' quality indicators in the repository (`data/standards.json`, dataset 1.2.0, verified 11 Oct 2026) say "completion of the mandatory NDIS worker orientation program" (core, human resource management) and "Workers complete mandatory NDIS orientation module" (verification module): the item's source line quotes the indicator and says "mandatory". VERIFY only the module's current title ("Quality, Safety and You" is the primer's name).
- W-5 Induction against the provider's own induction procedure (REQ-005), recorded in the induction and training register (REQ-018).
- W-6 Read-and-acknowledge of the mandatory policies: today's `policies-walked-through` item names the Code of Conduct, incident, complaints, privacy and safeguarding policies (`src/lib/registers/onboarding-items.ts`); that list is the "mandatory policies" everywhere this REQ says it. No acknowledgement rows exist today (`policy_circulations` is owner-recorded): the rows are new work in Session B.
- W-7 Qualifications and training the supports require (first aid, CPR, medication, manual handling, the high-intensity skills descriptors, PBS capability): derived from the account's modules; the product names what the module's quality indicators name and marks the rest "your call". VERIFY against the Practice Standards' quality indicators (REQ-001 mapping).
- W-8 Supervision and probation: the provider's own cadence (the governance and HR fixed text); the auditor expects a record that supervision happened; `supervision-due` already fires monthly from `staff_matrix.last_supervision_on` (REQ-018 FR-8) and stays the only supervision reminder.
- W-9 Restrictive practices: a worker who may use one needs the account's behaviour support arrangements and the state authorisation pathway in their induction (D-2223 item 3). VERIFY per state.
- W-10 Other checks a real provider holds: a police check and a driver licence for transport supports; dated `worker_clearances` rows (FR-5).

## Functional requirements (numbered FR-1 ...)

Session A, the onboarding sequence

- FR-1 `staff_matrix` is the worker row (no new `workers` table). It gains: `role` from a closed list (support worker, team leader, key personnel, admin, contractor (engaged by you), volunteer) beside the free `role_label`; `states text[]` (from the account's states); `works_with_children bool`; `may_use_restrictive_practice bool` (shown only when the account uses any); `ended_on date null`; `risk_assessed` stays and drives W-1. The link to a signed-in worker is by code: `memberships.worker_ref` (REQ-020 Session B) equals the matrix row's code; no `membership_id`. No name, no label, no date of birth, no address (D-2406); the code is the name, as today. Existing rows keep working; new columns default to empty. The role's source line says labour-hire personnel need the s 13(b) contract and the s 19 records, which this product does not hold.
- FR-2 The onboarding list is derived, not typed: `WORKER_ONBOARDING_ITEMS` (`src/lib/registers/onboarding-items.ts`) becomes a function of the worker's role, states and flags and the account's modules: for each applicable item from W-1 to W-10, who does it (owner, worker, both), what counts as done (a date, a number, an expiry, a tick with a date), the plain-words ask ("Show me this worker's clearance and the date you checked it in the database"), and a source line. The ids stay `item_id` with the existing CHECK. `onboarding_ticks` stays append-only as the dated evidence; the mutable state lives in a new `onboarding_items` row per (checklist, `item_id`) (Data): `done_on`, `confirmed_on`, `expires_on`, `number` (the batch 45 CHECK), `note` (capped); every change to it also appends one tick and one `register_events` row.
- FR-2a State facts live in `data/state-screening.json`: per state, the screening unit's name and URL, the read date, whether the state is "no card, no start" under s 14A, the acceptable checks it recognises, and the working with children check's name and URL. `onboarding-items.ts` reads it; an entry without a read date falls back to the national wording with "check your state's unit" (D-2404, unit-tested). The watch file records the reads; the data file is what the product shows.
- FR-3 The owner's views: `/registers/staff` (today's matrix page) lists workers with the derived state (ready for first shift, in progress, expiring, ended) and the next thing to do; the worker's page shows every item with its state and dates, the worker's clearances (REQ-018) and induction rows pulled in, and "Export this worker's file" (FR-6). Every change is a `register_events` row for `staff_matrix` or `onboarding_checklists`, append-only (REQ-009), with the kinds Data adds.
- FR-4 "Ready for first shift" is derived: every item marked "before first shift" (W-1 cleared or the W-1a exception running, W-2 where it applies, W-3, W-4, W-5, W-6 for the mandatory policies in W-6's list) is done, confirmed and not expired. The s 14 exception is offered only when the account has another worker with a current `ndis-worker-screening` row to supervise (a one-person account reads "You need your clearance before you work in this role", finding 28) and the worker's state is not "no card, no start" without an acceptable check (FR-2a; otherwise the item reads that state's wording). Starting it records four lines, each with a date, none optional: "the screening unit's written notice seen on <date>; application number <number> recorded; we confirmed to the unit that this person is our worker" (s 15(3), the application number required); "supervised by <worker code> (holds a current clearance)"; "our Division 3 risk management plan is adopted, dated <adoption date from the document control register>" (FR-4a); "our state allows it: <the state's wording from FR-2a>". The exception ends on the decision, on an interim bar, or when the owner records that supervision or the plan stopped (s 15(2)); the end date is required then. The product never offers "start without a check".
- FR-4a The Division 3 plan is a new section of the worker screening and recruitment procedure (REQ-005 document type), "Risk management plan for workers awaiting clearance", generated from the account's facts: the risks to the people the account supports while a worker is pending, how each is managed (supervision, no lone work, the tasks withheld), who reviews it and when (after any reportable incident involving a worker, s 12(3)). The document's adoption date is what FR-4's third line reads; until the procedure is adopted the exception cannot start.
- FR-5 Every dated check is a `worker_clearances` row: `clearance_kind` gains `cpr` and `driver-licence` beside today's kinds (`ndis-worker-screening`, `wwcc`, `first-aid`, `police-check`, `ndis-worker-orientation-module`, `manual-handling`, `medication`, `other`); the onboarding item reads that row's dates and number; the existing `clearance-expiry` reminders at 90, 30, 7 and 0 days are the only expiry reminders, and FR-11's entries update `last_supervision_on` so `supervision-due` stays the only supervision reminder. No new reminder path. First aid and CPR expiries are typed from the certificate; when empty, the product suggests 3 years and 1 year as "Readyline's suggestion" (one constant each with a comment, the D-2302 pattern) and the ask says "check the date on the certificate".
- FR-6 The worker file export: `workers/<code>.csv`, one per worker, plus `workers/index.csv`, in the audit pack's `workers/` folder, with every item, its state, dates, check and application numbers and the trail. One builder, two audiences: the owner's pack carries the numbers; the auditor link's pack (REQ-020 Session A serves the same zip behind the link) drops every number column from `workers/` and from the two Rules lists and never carries a fill (SR-8(a)); a test asserts the link's zip holds no check or application number column.
- FR-6a The two Rules lists as exports from the same data: `risk-assessed-roles.csv` (s 17: the role title, the definition paragraph as a code, the description, the date assessed, the assessor's code) and `screening-list.csv` (s 18: every current and past worker whose row is `risk_assessed`, by code, with the roles, the exception section and its start and end dates and the supervisor's code where one applied, the application number, the check number, the outcome and its expiry, and any decision from the closed list interim bar, suspension, revocation, other, each with its date and a capped note for its nature). The list is reconstructable for any day in the last seven years from the rows and the trail (s 20; AC-13). The name, date of birth and address columns, the assessor's name and title, and a supervisor's name are filled per D-2408 (FR-6b) or carry the bracket text.
- FR-6b The one-time fill (D-2408, scoped to third-party people by D-2409): the export screen for `screening-list.csv`, `risk-assessed-roles.csv` and the worker file carries one unticked checkbox, "Fill in names, dates of birth and addresses now. Readyline uses them for this file only and does not keep them.", and the one-line APP 5 notice with the policy link. Ticked, the screen shows one row per current and past worker code with three fields (full name, up to 120 characters; date of birth, an ISO date; address, up to 200), plus the assessor's name and title and each supervisor's name (120 each); every field is refused by `looks_like_ndis_number`; `autocomplete="off"`, no draft state, cleared on download, navigation or after ten minutes. Submitting posts `multipart/form-data` to the route handler `POST /registers/staff/export/screening-list` (and the sibling paths for the roles list and the worker file) behind the origin check, the member gate and the subscription gate; the handler builds the CSV in memory and answers `Content-Disposition: attachment` with `Cache-Control: no-store`; nothing is written anywhere (SR-7). Unticked, or for any cell left empty, the file carries `[Full name]`, `[Date of birth]`, `[Address]`, `[Assessor name and title]`, `[Supervisor name]` as plain bracket text (a CSV has no highlight; the REQ-016 highlight applies only if a docx worker file is ever produced), and the file's first line says these are for the owner to complete, with the s 17 or s 18 reference, that the owner keeps the file with the HR records for seven years (s 21), and that Readyline does not keep a copy.

## Security and privacy requirements (numbered SR-1 ...)

- SR-1 Workers are people: the product holds a code, a role, states, flags, dates, check and application numbers with the batch 45 CHECK, the invitation's email hash (REQ-020 Session B), and capped notes. No names, no labels, no dates of birth, no addresses, no identity documents, no certificates as files. The privacy policy diff is Session A's deliverable: "What we collect" names the email hash, the code, the role, the check and application numbers, the dates, the supervision and acknowledgement rows, and the fill fields as "used for one file, not kept", with the sentence that the register is the companion to the list the Worker Screening Rules ask the provider to keep, not the list itself; the collection notice is one screen with one button on the worker's first sign-in.
- SR-2 Policy path for the worker role: `onboarding_items.checklist_id -> onboarding_checklists (kind = 'worker', subject_ref) = memberships.worker_ref where memberships.user_id = auth.uid()`, and the same `account_id`; a worker may insert their own ticks and update only `done_on`, `number` and `expires_on` on their own `onboarding_items` rows, never `confirmed_on`; `onboarding_ticks` keeps no update policy; the owner reads and writes all rows in the account; the auditor link reads codes and states; integration tests for every policy and both roles in the Supabase job.
- SR-3 Every write is a `register_events` row with the actor's role and the time; nothing is deleted; ending a worker is an event.
- SR-4 Reminders name the worker by code and the item, never a number.
- SR-5 The owner's pack and exports carry codes and check numbers (the auditor verifies by number in the database); the auditor link's pack carries codes, states and the check number (D-2407 as amended 15 Oct 2026, FR-6).
- SR-6 The import refuses rows with a participant-shaped value in any field (REQ-020 FR-12 and the batch 45 CHECK), and the note field's helper says "no names".
- SR-7 The one-time fill (FR-6b) is a route handler whose body is used to render one response and discarded: no table, no `audit_log` payload (one line `exports.screening-list.filled` with the count of rows, no values), no server log of the body, no error report carrying the body (`redact.ts`'s key lists gain `full_name`, `date_of_birth`, `address`, `assessor_name`, `supervisor_name`), no query string, no cookie, no client-side storage, `Cache-Control: no-store`. Tests: a unit test on the handler asserts a marked value is in the response body and in none of the logger's captured lines; an integration test in the Supabase job asserts that after a filled download no table and no `audit_log` row holds the marked value.
- SR-8 Loopholes in the one-time fill, closed (the PO's legal and domain pass of 15 Oct 2026, D-2222): (a) a filled file is a direct download only; it is never written into a stored pack, the auditor link's pack, a cache or an email attachment, and the handler refuses a fill from any path but the export screen; (b) the fill form has `autocomplete="off"` on every field, is never part of the questionnaire draft store or any client-side state that persists, and is cleared on download, navigation or after ten minutes; (c) a half-filled form produces bracket text for the empty cells, never a silent blank; (d) the handler's error path writes the count-only audit line and returns a plain message, so no body reaches a log or an error report; (e) the values are processed where the function runs, so the privacy policy's overseas line names the region (REQ-022 D-2201's VERIFY on syd1 applies here too); (f) the file's first line says: "Keep this file with your HR records for seven years (Worker Screening Rules s 21). Readyline does not keep a copy."; (g) the product never claims the owner "has" the s 17 or s 18 list: the wording is "this becomes your list once you complete and keep it"; (h) the assessor's name and title (s 17) and a supervising worker's name (s 18(3)(c)(iii)) are fill fields on the same screen, same rules; (i) the fill is a collection under APP 3 even without storage, so the APP 5 notice sits on the screen itself in one line with the link to the policy; (j) the SR-7 tests prove nothing remains and that a second download without the tick carries bracket text only.
