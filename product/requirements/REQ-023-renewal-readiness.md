# REQ-023: Renewal and mid-term audit readiness: the registration timeline, "since the last audit", the renewal path, and the changes register

Status: ready (PO, 16 Oct 2026 02:50; fourth version; the second pass of the re-review, headings 7 and 8 under D-2222, `product/qa/req-reviews/2026-10-15-req-023-rereview.md` findings 28 to 55, verdict "Ready with edits", every finding applied below; Session A can start once `next` carries #62; Sessions B to D wait for REQ-020 to be on `next`)
Phase: 6 Priority: should Owner: Dev

## Why (user and business outcome)

The subscription keeps four things for a registered provider: the registers, the reminders, the mid-term audit countdown and the audit pack. Today the product knows a registration date and sends a handful of reminders from it, but it has no timeline a provider can read, no way to show an auditor "the period since the last audit", and no record of what was told to the Commission. A registered provider lives on a clock: a mid-term audit for most certification providers that must start by month 18, a window of six months before the period ends in which an application keeps the registration in force while the Commission decides (NDIS Act s 73K), and a period end that ends the registration if no such application is in. This REQ gives the account a timeline it can read, filters the registers and the audit pack by period, turns the quarterly self-check into a rehearsal, and keeps the one register nobody keeps: the changes told to the Commission. Every sentence this REQ adds names one of those mechanisms and never a state: the product cannot make anyone audit-ready, the provider does, and the words on screen say what the product did (ACL s 18 and s 4; finding 28, D-2307).

## Scope (in / out)

In, as four sessions:

- Session A, the registration timeline: dates the provider enters once, the dates the product derives, and the reminders that come from them (replacing the ones `reminders.ts` sends today).
- Session B, "since the last audit": a period filter on the registers, the exports and the audit pack, and a "what changed" summary. After REQ-020.
- Session C, the renewal path: a guided page for the six months before expiry, with the quarterly self-check as its rehearsal. After REQ-020.
- Session D, the changes register: what was notified to the Commission and when, and what was applied for as a variation. After REQ-020.

Out: anything that applies for renewal on the provider's behalf or talks to the Commission's portal; auditor selection or booking (CLAUDE.md 4.4); a second subscription tier; worker onboarding (REQ-024); showing which documents a changed answer set would change (a document diff; a changed answer set is a new pack); the annual declaration (UNVERIFIED as an obligation with a date; it goes on the timeline when the primer records a source).

## Domain rules that apply (link to product/domain)

`CLAUDE.md` 4.2 (no urgency: a date is a fact, never a countdown in red), 4.4 (no referral role), 4.5 (no participant data; nothing in this REQ asks for any). REQ-009 (append-only registers, plain words; `accounts.registration_date` and `registration_kind`), REQ-017 (one screen, one action), REQ-018 (review cycles, audit-day list, the reminders job and its settings), REQ-020 (audit pack, auditor link, self-check, import).

- R-1 Registration is in force for "the period for which the registration is in force" stated on the certificate (NDIS Act s 73E(5)(e), compilation 28 C2026C00451, read 16 Oct 2026 by the reviewer); three years is usual; the Commissioner may "reduce or extend" that period (s 73L(3)(b)). Both dates, the day the period starts and the day it ends, are typed from the certificate, never pre-filled or assumed (finding 30). The day the Commissioner granted the registration and the first day of the period can differ; the product uses the period's first day everywhere (finding 54).
- R-2 For a certification provider the mid-term audit "must commence no later than (a) 18 months after the beginning of the period for which the provider's registration is in force; or (b) such longer period after the beginning of that period as the Commissioner allows" (Rules F2026C00527 s 13B(4), read 16 Oct 2026; `product/domain/ndis-registration-primer.md` line 20; `MID_TERM_DUE_MONTHS = 18` in `src/lib/registers/reminders.ts`). A registration condition can also set the timing of audits (Act s 73G(3)(b)); where the certificate's conditions or a Commissioner's letter give a date, that date rules (finding 44). The mid-term audit does not apply to every certification provider: s 13B(7) exempts a partnership or individual whose only class is early childhood early intervention, a provider whose only class is specialist disability accommodation, and a transitioned provider; `src/lib/standards/derive.ts` already derives this as `midTermAudit.applies` with an `exemptReason` (`derive.test.ts:84-114`), and that derivation, never the audit type alone, decides whether a mid-term line, item or reminder exists (finding 45). Verification providers have no mid-term audit. The audit assesses the Part 3 standards, any standard under a corrective action plan from the last audit, and any standard the Commissioner names in a notice (s 13B(5)); the report goes to the Commissioner "as soon as practicable after the audit is completed" (s 13B(6)); the 28-day figure belongs to the change-of-ownership audit only (s 13BA(6)) and never reaches the timeline from a snippet (finding 53).
- R-3 The Act has no renewal provision: every application is one under s 73C, and "renewal" is the Commission's word for a s 73C application by a provider who is already registered (finding 42). What distinguishes an application made in the window is s 73K (read 16 Oct 2026, compilation 28): s 73K(1) "Subsection (2) applies if: (a) the registration of a person as a registered NDIS provider will cease to be in force on a particular day; and (b) within 6 months before that day, the person makes an application under section 73C." s 73K(2) "Despite paragraph 73E(5)(e), the registration of the person continues in force until the Commissioner makes a decision on the application under subsection 73E(1)." So "Your renewal window opens" is the earliest day an application keeps the registration in force while the Commission decides, and an application that is not with the Commission before the period ends leaves the registration to end on that day (findings 32 and 41); the Act never says "lapses". The Commission's practice order, read by the PO on 15 Oct 2026 at https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/renew-your-registration, https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/apply-registration and the portal guide https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/P19-How%20to%20renew%20my%20registration.pdf (the reviewers' fetches timed out, so it stays UNVERIFIED until a second read with quotes goes into the watch file; where a page and the Rules differ, the Rules' words go on screen and the page is practice, finding 53): the application is submitted in the portal first; the Commission then emails the scope of audit; the provider engages an approved quality auditor and the audit happens; the Commission reviews and decides; a certificate follows. The audit is therefore after the application, not before it. "Lapsed" appears in this REQ only as the Commission's own word, quoted.
- R-4 Notifications to the Commission under s 13 and s 13A of the Rules carry no day count; the Rule texts, read 16 Oct 2026, are three (finding 48): s 13(2)(b), notice of a s 13 change "as soon as practicable after the earlier of ... (i) when the provider becomes aware that the change will occur; (ii) when the change occurs"; s 13A(4), notice of a s 13A event "by the earlier of ... (a) when the provider becomes aware that the event or change will occur; (b) when the event or change occurs"; s 13A(3), a change of ownership "by the earlier of ... (a) when the provider becomes aware that the change in ownership will occur; (b) when a step or procedure occurs that is a necessary precondition to effecting the change of ownership". So a s 13 change is "to notify" from the earlier of awareness and the change, and never "overdue" by a day count; a s 13A event has a notify-by date that is the earlier of awareness and the event; a change of ownership's is the earlier of awareness and the first necessary step. The s 13 list is contact details, the area, locations, and participant and worker numbers (`data/watch-changes.json` line 158); "significant" in s 13(1)(b)(iii) and (iv) is defined nowhere in the Rules (finding 50). A change of the supports or services registered is a variation of registration: an application under s 73M, decided under s 73L(3)(c), taking effect "on the day specified in the notice" (s 73L(6)), with a varied certificate (s 73L(4)); the Commissioner may also vary conditions (s 73L(3)(a)) or the period (s 73L(3)(b)), which reach this product as FR-1 edits (findings 35 and 43). Key personnel: a director or manager joining or leaving is, if significant, a s 13A(1)(d) change in organisation or governance arrangements; a suitability event is s 13A(1)(f) (finding 49); the Commission's page on reporting key personnel is UNVERIFIED (https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/manage-your-registration, timed out 16 Oct 2026). A change of ownership by a certification provider adds a certification audit that "must commence no later than (a) 3 months after the day on which the relevant change occurs; or (b) such longer period ... as the Commissioner allows", unless a s 13B audit is under way or the registration ends within those three months (s 13BA; finding 47). Notices are given "in the form approved by the Commissioner" (s 13(2)(a), s 13A(2)); the approved form could not be read (UNVERIFIED: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/notify-us-changes-or-events, timed out 16 Oct 2026; a search snippet, not a source, says the new portal takes notifications from 12 October 2026).
- R-5 An auditor at renewal samples documents and registers across the registration period, with weight on the period since the last audit (the webinar research of 14 Oct; practice, not a rule). The audit pack therefore shows the whole period by default and the since-last-audit view as a summary.
- R-6 Records: the Rules carry no record-keeping period for s 13 and s 13A notices; the 7-year rules are the Incident Rules s 25, the Complaints Rules s 10(3) and the Worker Screening Rules s 21, and the Act s 73R requires a person who has ceased to be registered to keep the s 73Q records for 3 years from the day they ceased (findings 40 and 51).

## Acceptance criteria (Given/When/Then, numbered AC-1 ...)

- AC-1 Given a certification provider whose certificate's period starts 1 March 2026 and ends 1 March 2029 (both typed; the form refuses to save with the end date empty), `midterm_applies` true, and a fixed clock of 15 October 2026, when they open the account page, then the timeline reads: prepare from 1 March 2027 (our suggestion); must start by 1 September 2027 (Rules s 13B); renewal window opens 1 September 2028 (NDIS Act s 73K); apply by 1 December 2028 (our suggestion); registration period ends 1 March 2029 (your certificate); the source words are asserted; the fixed sentence under the table is present; and the position sentence reads "You are 7 months in. Prepare for your mid-term audit from 1 March 2027; it must start by 1 September 2027, unless your certificate's conditions say otherwise." With an override date of 1 December 2027 entered, the must-start-by row reads 1 December 2027 (your certificate's conditions).
