# REQ-023: Renewal and mid-term audit readiness: the registration timeline, "since the last audit", the renewal path, and the changes register

Status: ready (PO, 16 Oct 2026 11:10; fifth version; the third pass, `product/qa/req-reviews/2026-10-16-req-023-third-pass.md` findings 56 to 90, verdict "Ready with edits", every finding applied below and the five decisions it asked for taken as D-2312 to D-2315 and D-2302's note; Session A starts once #69 is on `next`, and the decisions are also in CHAT 16 Oct 10:35 so the build does not wait for this text; Sessions B to D wait for REQ-020 to be on `next`)
Phase: 6 Priority: should Owner: Dev

## Why (user and business outcome)

The subscription keeps four things for a registered provider: the registers, the reminders, the mid-term audit countdown and the audit pack. Today the product knows a registration date and sends a handful of reminders from it, but it has no timeline a provider can read, no way to show an auditor "the period since the last audit", and no record of what was told to the Commission. A registered provider lives on a clock: a mid-term audit for most certification providers that must start by month 18, a window of six months before the period ends in which an application keeps the registration in force while the Commission decides (NDIS Act s 73K), and a period end that ends the registration if no such application is in. This REQ gives the account a timeline it can read, filters the registers and the audit pack by period, turns the quarterly self-check into a rehearsal, and keeps the one register nobody keeps: the changes told to the Commission. Every sentence this REQ adds names one of those mechanisms and never a state: the product cannot make anyone audit-ready, the provider does, and the words on screen say what the product did (ACL s 18 and s 4; finding 28, D-2307).

## Scope (in / out)

In, as four sessions:

- Session A, the registration timeline: dates the provider enters once, the dates the product derives, and the reminders that come from them (replacing the ones `reminders.ts` sends today).
- Session B, "since the last audit": a period filter on the registers, the exports and the audit pack, and a "what changed" summary. After REQ-020.
- Session C, the renewal path: a guided page for the six months before expiry, with the quarterly self-check as its rehearsal. After REQ-020.
- Session D, the changes register: what was notified to the Commission and when, and what was applied for as a variation. After REQ-020.

Out: anything that applies for renewal on the provider's behalf or talks to the Commission's portal; auditor selection or booking (CLAUDE.md 4.4); a second subscription tier; worker onboarding (REQ-024); showing which documents a changed answer set would change (a document diff; a changed answer set is a new pack); the annual declaration (UNVERIFIED as an obligation with a date; it goes on the timeline when the primer records a source).

- R-1 Registration is in force for "the period for which the registration is in force" stated on the certificate (NDIS Act s 73E(5)(e), compilation 28 C2026C00451, read 16 Oct 2026 by the reviewer); three years is usual; the Commissioner may "reduce or extend" that period (s 73L(3)(b)). Both dates, the day the period starts and the day it ends, are typed from the certificate, never pre-filled or assumed (finding 30). The day the Commissioner granted the registration and the first day of the period can differ; the product uses the period's first day everywhere (finding 54).
- R-2 For a certification provider the mid-term audit "must commence no later than (a) 18 months after the beginning of the period for which the provider's registration is in force; or (b) such longer period after the beginning of that period as the Commissioner allows" (Rules F2026C00527 s 13B(4), read 16 Oct 2026; `product/domain/ndis-registration-primer.md` line 20; `MID_TERM_DUE_MONTHS = 18` in `src/lib/registers/reminders.ts`). A registration condition can also set the timing of audits (Act s 73G(3)(b)); where the certificate's conditions or a Commissioner's letter give a date, that date rules (finding 44). The mid-term audit does not apply to every certification provider: s 13B(7) exempts a partnership or individual whose only certification class is "early intervention supports for early childhood" (s 13B(7)(a)(ii); a verification class beside it does not remove the exemption, and `derive.ts`'s `onlyEcIsCertification` reads it that way; finding 70), a provider whose only class is specialist disability accommodation, and a transitioned provider; `src/lib/standards/derive.ts` derives the first two as `midTermAudit.applies` with an `exemptReason` (`derive.test.ts:84-114`); the transitioned-provider exemption has no input in the questionnaire and is not modelled (D-2315; finding 71): a provider the Commission registered as transitioned under the Transitional Rules 2018 reads the FR-1 reason line, which says so, and enters the Commissioner's date in the override field if one was given. The derivation and FR-1's audit type together, never the audit type alone, decide whether a mid-term line, item or reminder exists (findings 45 and 78). Verification providers have no mid-term audit (s 13B(1) attaches the condition to a provider registered for a class whose assessment method under the s 20(3) table is certification; the test is the classes on the certificate, finding 87). The audit is "carried out by an approved quality auditor using certification" (s 13B(2) and (3); finding 90), so FR-4's "Mid-term audit started" item asks for the auditing company's name as FR-7's booking step does. The audit assesses the Part 3 standards, any standard under a corrective action plan from the last audit, and any standard the Commissioner names in a notice (s 13B(5)); the report goes to the Commissioner "as soon as practicable after the audit is completed" (s 13B(6)); the 28-day figure belongs to the change-of-ownership audit only (s 13BA(6)) and never reaches the timeline from a snippet (finding 53).
- R-3 The Act has no renewal provision: every application is one under s 73C, and "renewal" is the Commission's word for a s 73C application by a provider who is already registered (finding 42). What distinguishes an application made in the window is s 73K (read 16 Oct 2026, compilation 28): s 73K(1) "Subsection (2) applies if: (a) the registration of a person as a registered NDIS provider will cease to be in force on a particular day; and (b) within 6 months before that day, the person makes an application under section 73C." s 73K(2) "Despite paragraph 73E(5)(e), the registration of the person continues in force until the Commissioner makes a decision on the application under subsection 73E(1)." So "Your renewal window opens" is the earliest day an application keeps the registration in force while the Commission decides, and an application that is not with the Commission before the period ends leaves the registration to end on that day (findings 32 and 41); the Act never says "lapses". The Commission's practice order, read by the PO on 15 Oct 2026 and by the third-pass reviewer on 16 Oct 2026 with quotes (finding 68; where a page and the Rules differ, the Rules' words go on screen and the page is practice, finding 53): https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/renew-your-registration says "6 months before your registration expiry date", "If you start before your expiry date your current registration will remain valid until we make a decision" (wider than s 73K's "within 6 months before"; the Act's words rule), "A lapsed registration is caused by your registration expiring or by being deregistered", "If your registration has lapsed, you will need to complete another application and audit process", the renewal "follows the same steps" as the application including "self-assessment against the applicable NDIS practice standards", a renewal under the same ABN goes through the portal and a new ABN "is viewed as a new application" (finding 88); https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/apply-registration says "When you submit your application, you will get an email with an Initial scope of audit document", the steps run "Apply online", "Get audited", "Application review", "We'll let you know our decision", "Get your certificate of registration", and "You must complete your application within 60 days of starting it or it will be deleted" (whether the 60 days binds a renewal started in "My applications" is not stated: UNVERIFIED, finding 89); the portal guide https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/P19-How%20to%20renew%20my%20registration.pdf is portal navigation only ("If your registration is in its final 6 months, open My applications and start the formal renewal"). So: the application is submitted in the portal first; the Commission then emails the scope of audit; the provider engages an approved quality auditor and the audit happens; the Commission reviews and decides; a certificate follows. The audit is therefore after the application, not before it. The types-of-audits page stays UNVERIFIED (https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/apply-registration/types-audits, robots-disallowed 16 Oct 2026). "Lapsed" appears in this REQ only as the Commission's own word, quoted above.
- R-4 Notifications to the Commission under s 13 and s 13A of the Rules carry no day count; the Rule texts, read 16 Oct 2026, are three (finding 48): s 13(2)(b), notice of a s 13 change "as soon as practicable after the earlier of ... (i) when the provider becomes aware that the change will occur; (ii) when the change occurs"; s 13A(4), notice of a s 13A event "by the earlier of ... (a) when the provider becomes aware that the event or change will occur; (b) when the event or change occurs"; s 13A(3), a change of ownership "by the earlier of ... (a) when the provider becomes aware that the change in ownership will occur; (b) when a step or procedure occurs that is a necessary precondition to effecting the change of ownership". So a s 13 change is "to notify" from the earlier of awareness and the change, and never "overdue" by a day count; a s 13A event has a notify-by date that is the earlier of awareness and the event; a change of ownership's is the earlier of awareness and the first necessary step. The s 13 list is contact details, the area, locations, and participant and worker numbers (`data/watch-changes.json` line 158); "significant" in s 13(1)(b)(iii) and (iv) is defined nowhere in the Rules (finding 50). A change of the supports or services registered is a variation of registration: an application under s 73M, decided under s 73L(3)(c), taking effect "on the day specified in the notice" (s 73L(6)), with a varied certificate (s 73L(4)); the Commissioner may also vary conditions (s 73L(3)(a)) or the period (s 73L(3)(b)), which reach this product as FR-1 edits (findings 35 and 43). Key personnel: a director or manager joining or leaving is, if significant, a s 13A(1)(d) change in organisation or governance arrangements; a suitability event is s 13A(1)(f) (finding 49); the Commission's page https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/notify-us-changes-or-events, read 16 Oct 2026 by the reviewer (finding 69), lists "your key personnel (such as a new CEO or company director)" among the changes to notify, with contact details, locations, services, service areas, legal structure, names and ownership, the route "Log in to the NDIS Commission registered providers portal", and the timing "You must notify us in the registered providers portal when the change occurs or as soon as possible afterwards" (practice; the Rules' "earlier of" stays on screen). A change of ownership by a certification provider adds a certification audit that "must commence no later than (a) 3 months after the day on which the relevant change occurs; or (b) such longer period ... as the Commissioner allows", unless "(i) the provider is not undergoing an audit described in section 13B; and (ii) the registration of the provider does not cease to be in force" fail, that is, a s 13B audit is under way or the registration ends within those three months (s 13BA(1)(c), both limbs; findings 47 and 86). Notices are given "in the form approved by the Commissioner" (s 13(2)(a), s 13A(2)); the notify page names the portal as that form. A variation is applied for by email: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/provider-registration/manage-your-registration (read 16 Oct 2026, finding 73) says "Email variations@ndiscommission.gov.au to receive an 'Application for Variation of Registration' form" and the outcome is "a notice of decision and an updated Certificate of Registration (if applicable)"; re-read when Session D starts.
- R-5 An auditor at renewal samples documents and registers across the registration period, with weight on the period since the last audit (the webinar research of 14 Oct; practice, not a rule). The audit pack therefore shows the whole period by default and the since-last-audit view as a summary.
- R-6 Records: the Rules carry no record-keeping period for s 13 and s 13A notices; the 7-year rules are the Incident Rules s 25, the Complaints Rules s 10(3) and the Worker Screening Rules s 21, and the Act s 73R requires a person who has ceased to be registered to keep the s 73Q records for 3 years from the day they ceased (findings 40 and 51).
