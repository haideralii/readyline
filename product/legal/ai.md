# How Readyline uses AI

## What is written by people and what is drafted by AI

Every document Readyline produces has two kinds of text.

Fixed text is written and reviewed by people. It holds the parts that must be exact: the Commission's standards and quality indicators, the reportable incident rules and timeframes, definitions, register layouts, and the review sections. Fixed text is the same for every provider and does not come from an AI model.

Tailored text is drafted by an AI model from the answers you give in the questionnaire: your trading name, your business structure, the states you work in, the supports you deliver, how many workers you have, and whether you use restrictive practices. The model writes sections such as the scope, the policy statement, how you put the policy into practice, and who does what.

Every document shows which sections are which.

## Which model, and where your data goes

We use Claude, a model made by Anthropic. When we draft a document, we send the model your questionnaire answers and the fixed text for that document. We do not send participant information, because we never collect it. Anthropic does not use our requests to train its models under its commercial terms, as read on {{vendor-terms-date}}.

## How we check the drafts

Each tailored section is checked by software before you see it: it must be about your business facts only, use plain words at about a Year 8 reading level, use the Commission's terms, avoid claims such as "guaranteed" or "audit-proof", and refer to the fixed sections for legal timeframes rather than restating them. A section that fails these checks is redrafted once; if it still fails, you see a clear marker asking you to complete that section yourself.

## Your own statement on AI and digital tools

Your pack does not carry a separate AI policy. Your statement on AI and digital tools, which tools you allow and for what, that no participant information goes into public tools, and who reviews what a tool produces, is an appendix to the privacy policy in your pack, so it sits with your privacy commitments, where an auditor would look for it. You find it with your other documents on [your documents page](/documents).

## What you must do

The documents are drafts for your review. You are responsible for reading them, changing anything that is not true for your business, and adopting them. Readyline does not give legal advice and does not guarantee that you will pass an audit or be registered.

## Telling us about a wrong output

If a draft says something wrong, odd or unclear, email us at the contact address in the footer with the document name and the section. We read every report and use them to improve the checks.
