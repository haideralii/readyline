# Privacy policy

## Who we are

Readyline is run by a sole trader in Sydney, Australia. {{business-line}} Contact details are in the footer.

## What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

- Your account email address, so you can sign in and we can send receipts and service emails.
- Your questionnaire answers: trading name, ABN if you give it, business structure, states and territories, the supports you deliver, your number of workers and key personnel, whether you use restrictive practices, and which documents you already have.
- The documents we draft for you and the notes you add to your checklist or registers.
- Audit findings you type or paste, in your own words, with the standard, the severity and the dates you record. A report you upload is read in Sydney to pick out its findings and is not kept, logged or sent to a model. Your findings are in your account export and are deleted with your account.
- The gap check's list, if you compare a policy you already have with Readyline's outline: the document type, the title you give it, and the state and reason for each outline section. The document itself is read once and not kept.
- If you ask us to email you your result, we send it once to the address you type and do not keep the address. We keep a one-way code of it in our security log so we can see a send happened.
- Your name, your role and the phone number or email address you give us for your documents. We put them into every document you download, into your audit pack and into the auditor link you share, and we keep them while your account is open.
- Worker codes, each worker's role and the states they work in, the kind of check, its dates, the application number and, if you choose, the check number you enter in the worker checks register, the onboarding items with their dates and who recorded them, the supervision dates on the staff matrix, and the hashed email address of a worker you invite. We ask you to use codes for workers. We never keep a worker's name, date of birth or address, so the register is the companion to the list the Worker Screening Rules ask you to keep, not the list itself. If you choose to fill names, dates of birth and addresses into an export, we use them to build that one file in Sydney and do not keep them.
- Purchase records: what you bought, when, the amount, and the payment reference. We never see or store your card number; our payment provider, Stripe, handles it.
- Basic technical records such as the time of a sign-in and errors, kept to run and secure the service. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

## What we never collect

Readyline is for your business information only. Do not enter personal, health or disability information about NDIS participants anywhere in Readyline, including in notes and registers. Use a code or reference number instead of a name. If we find participant information, we may remove it and tell you.

## How we use your information

- To draft your documents and run your checklist and registers.
- To take payment and send receipts.
- To send you service emails such as a sign-in link, a "your documents are ready" email, and reminders that come with your subscription, which you can turn off. We do not send marketing email unless you opt in, and you can opt out at any time.
- To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, or where the law requires us to.

## Who helps us run the service

- Supabase stores your account and data on servers in Sydney, Australia.
- Vercel runs the website. Its servers are in Sydney where we can choose, and otherwise in the United States. Never the worker-detail fill, which runs in Sydney.
- Anthropic provides the AI model that drafts your documents. We send it your questionnaire answers and the fixed text of the document. It does not use our requests to train its models under its commercial terms, as read on {{vendor-terms-date}}. Anthropic processes our requests in the United States.
- Stripe takes payments and keeps card details. Stripe may process data in the United States and elsewhere under its own privacy policy.
- GitHub, in the United States, keeps a weekly encrypted copy of our database for up to 90 days; only the owner holds the key that opens it, and each copy is deleted after 90 days.
- Sentry receives reports of errors on our servers, with email addresses and free text removed before they leave Australia, and stores them in the United States.
- An email provider sends service emails.

## How long we keep it

Your data stays while your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove your documents, answers and notes within 30 days, and keep only the purchase records the tax law requires us to keep, for the period it requires. An encrypted backup copy may still hold your data for up to 90 days after that, then it is deleted.

## Your choices

- Export: you can download everything we hold about you from your account page at any time: your account, your answers, your checklist, your registers (including the worker tables) and your audit log.
- Delete: you can delete your account from your account page. We confirm by email.
- Correct: you can change your answers and regenerate your documents.
- If you are a worker whose provider uses Readyline: your worker home shows what we hold about you, except your provider's own notes. Ask us by email for anything else; we answer within a reasonable period, and if we refuse we say why in writing. Your worker code and your check and application numbers go to your manager's auditor through a time-limited link, into your manager's own export of their account, and to the Commission if it asks. Your manager may type your name, date of birth and address, and the name of whoever supervises you, into a one-time file that Readyline builds and does not keep.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each account can only see its own data, enforced in the database. Sign-in uses a one-time email link; administrators use a second factor. We log security events and review them. If a breach happens that is likely to cause serious harm, we tell you, and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the law requires it.

## The Privacy Act

Some small businesses are not covered by the Privacy Act 1988. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles anyway, because that is what our customers expect and what the Commission expects of providers. Readyline has not registered a choice to be covered by the Privacy Act; what this page promises is our own undertaking to you, and the Australian Consumer Law holds us to it.

## Complaints and contact

Email us at the contact address in the footer. We reply within five business days. The owner decides and tells you the outcome in writing. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
