# About Readyline

## Why Readyline exists

Registering as an NDIS provider means passing an audit against the NDIS Practice Standards. For a sole trader or a small team, the hardest part is not the audit itself. It is working out which standards apply, which documents you need, and writing policies that describe your business rather than a template's idea of a business. Consultants charge thousands for this. Template packs are cheap but generic.

Readyline sits in between. You answer plain questions about what you do. Readyline tells you which audit type and standards the Rules point to for your answers, drafts the documents to fit your answers, and shows you what is still missing. You review and adopt the documents. When you are registered, the registers help you stay ready for the mid-term audit.

## Who runs it

Readyline is built and run by one person in Sydney, a software quality engineer who tests systems for a living. {{business-line}} It is not a law firm and not a consultancy.

## Independence

Readyline is independent of the National Disability Insurance Agency and the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission. We are not an approved quality auditor and never will be. We do not take referral fees or commissions from auditors, and if we ever list auditors, the list will be free and we will say so.

## How we earn money

One fixed price for the document pack, and an optional monthly subscription for the registers. No hidden fees, no instalments, no interest, no late fees. We do not sell your information and we do not run advertising.

## Where your data lives

Your account and your documents live in Sydney. The model that drafts the documents and the payment provider process requests overseas; see the Privacy policy. We never collect information about NDIS participants.
